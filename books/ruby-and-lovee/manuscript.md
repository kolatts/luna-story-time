# Ruby and Lovee

*Book Six of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna and Sunny.*

**Refrain:** Every heart holds something to love. (Spreads 4, 8, 12, 16.)

## 1. The wedding garden

Ruby was fifteen days old. In the sun,
her seven small dragons came out for some fun.
Through the wedding-garden arch, where the roses grew red,
where people said, "I love you," and got married, she led.

Then her silver mark tingled. She stopped by a chair.
"Oh! A pink egg with red hearts! Who tucked you in there?"

## 2. A gulp at the gate

At the gate, Black Hole landed with Noir and with War.
"Another new egg?" Noir cried. "One dragon more!
Gulp it up! Bring it here! We shall have it this time!"
Black Hole opened his swirly dark gulp with a whine.

The egg rocked toward Ruby. She reached out to hold.
Black Hole touched it first with his nose, dark and cold.

## 3. A heart went POP

Something hiccupped inside it. A heart-light went POP!
Black Hole shut his gulp with a fluttery flop.
He blinked up at Ruby. His scowl slipped away.
"She's kind," thought the dragon. "I'd like her to stay."

He brought her a rose and a cushion, then three,
and rolled on his back. "Is that REALLY you?" said she.

## 4. Ruby, too

Ruby lifted the egg. Then a second heart flew.
It brushed past her cheek with a warm little whoo.
She looked at Black Hole, at his soft, floppy ear.
"There's a nice bit in YOU!" And she scooted up near.

She scratched his chin gently. He waggled his feet.
"A friend!" Ruby giggled. "A friend I can meet!"

## 5. Too many presents

He fetched her a teapot. He fetched her a shoe.
He fetched a whole curtain, still tied to it too!
"I love how you TRY," Ruby laughed, "but, you see,
one rose and a place at my side work for me."

He dropped all his presents. He sat with a thump.
She leaned on his shoulder, that warm, lumpy lump.

## 6. OUR dragon

"Black Hole!" shouted War. "You come HERE when I call!"
"Fetch the egg!" ordered Noir. "Not the curtains! Not ALL!"
But he tucked his dark wing round the bench like a wall.
Ruby said, "He can sit. There's a place for us all."

"That egg has a POWER," Noir whispered to War.
"We'll take it. Then WE'LL be the ones they adore!"

## 7. Seven little helpers

Flame warmed the tea. Pearl blew bubbles in rows.
Leaf coaxed a green vine round the droopiest rose.
Sparks lit one lantern. Crackle lit two.
Luna made moonbeams the ribbons shone through.

Shinebright watched hearts drift away from the shell.
"It's helping you notice," she said. "I can tell."

## 8. Already inside

"Love doesn't put kindness where nothing has been.
It opens a window to what's tucked within."
"He can be gentle," said Ruby. "I see!"
Black Hole brought a dropped baby bird to a tree.

He set it down softly, then backed out of sight.
The bird fluffed its feathers. Its mama held tight.

## 9. A velvet bag

Noir crept to the bench with a bag, soft and black.
War held it wide open. "Pop in! In the sack!"
But Leaf's little vine caught the bag by its string,
and Pearl blew a bubble round egg, bow, and everything.

Sparks and Crackle went ZIP! to the bench, side by side.
They nudged the round bubble toward Ruby to hide.

## 10. A little less pink

Then the hearts grew much smaller. Their glow grew quite thin.
Black Hole blinked. Ruby felt something shift deep within.
"I still like your rose," she said, looking around,
"but my feet want to RUN now, not sit on the ground!"

He scuffed at a pebble. He looked toward the gate.
"The magic is fading," said Shinebright. "Just wait."

## 11. The ribbon slips

"It's wearing off! QUICK!" Noir reached for the nest.
She tugged at the ribbon beneath it. "The BEST!"
Off slid the cushion! The egg tipped out too!
Luna stretched out a moonbeam. Flame puffed, "Phew! Phew!"

But Black Hole was nearest. He could gulp it away.
Noir pointed. "Now GET IT!" What would he do? Stay?

## 12. His own small choice

The last little heart-light went out in the air.
Black Hole sprang forward. He WAS already there!
He caught it, all safe, in the curve of his wing,
and carried it gently. A small, careful thing.

He set it in Ruby's two hands, warm and tight.
No spell made him do it. He chose to do right.

## 13. Lovee

A knock from inside it. A tap. Then a CRACK!
Two tiny pink wings pushed the shell gently back.
Out tumbled Lovee, with her pink little head,
and her chest and her belly a warm cherry red.

Pink over her tail, with red underneath,
red soles on her feet, and a grin full of teeth.
"Hello, little Lovee!" Ruby whispered. "You're here."
Lovee snuggled her wing to Black Hole's floppy ear.

## 14. A pink little bond

Lovee pressed her small nose to Ruby's palm. Zing!
Ruby's dress turned rose pink with a red-hearted swing:
two heart-shaped red pockets, red trim at the seam,
and her hands held small heart-lights, as warm as a dream.

She cupped them. "Not now. We can choose what to do."
Then she opened her arms. Lovee chose her arms too.

## 15. Something to love

"I love a good grumble," War grumbled. "So THERE!"
"I love MY fine tower," said Noir, nose in air.
"I love little birds," Black Hole mumbled down low.
"And a rose?" Ruby asked. "Mm. Perhaps. I don't know."

She gave War a ribbon. "For something you love."
War tied up her braid, with a bow just above.

## 16. Eight at home

Home went Black Hole, with the two, through the gate.
He paused by the bird's nest. Then left. It was late.
Ruby's eight little dragons curled close, head to head,
with Lovee's pink wings like a quilt on the bed.

"I liked him," said Ruby. "I still like that part."
She kept his one rose by her pillow, near her heart.
And beyond the far hills, in a tower tall and steep,
a grumbly black dragon dreamed birds in his sleep.
