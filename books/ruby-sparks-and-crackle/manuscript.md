# Ruby, Sparks and Crackle

*Book Two of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna, Sunny, and Mommy.*

**Refrain:** *Good things hatch when they're ready.* Placed on spreads 3, 9, 16.

**Moral:** good things hatch when they're ready. You can't shout, grab, or race something into being ready, and that includes yourself. Noir and War want the egg *first* and want it *now*; Ruby holds it, hums, and waits, and the twins hatch for the one who was patient. For the grown-up: Ruby is eight days old and still can't reach the floor from the big chair. She'll grow into it when she's ready inside, not when the throne demands.

## Canon and liberties

- **Retcon (applies to Book One too):** there is no king. Ruby's papa has passed away; that is backstory only and is never said on the page. Noir is her stepmother, War her stepsister. The cook is the grown-up who helps Ruby learn.
- The Thunder Egg has always been in the hilltop pond, between Riverlight and Noir's kingdom. The 'thunder' that rolled low at the end of Book One was the egg rumbling.
- Ruby's forehead mark tingles when a dragon egg is ready, or nearly ready, nearby.
- Flame and Pearl stay home. A bond travels with you: Ruby uses Pearl's water magic (blue dress, a breathing bubble) without Pearl there.
- Sparks (yellow scales, silver eyes) and Crackle (silver scales, yellow eyes) are twin girls. They share **one** bond with Ruby, and her lightning dress is half yellow, half silver.
- Lightning in water: the twins' sparks can't hurt anyone they've chosen, and they choose Ruby the moment they hatch. To her it only tingles.
- War found her left boot (lost in Book One's sneeze).
- Nobody is hurt. Noir and War go home soaked.

## 1. Eight days old

Ruby was eight days old. She had counted each day.  
She could button her boots. She could put toys away.  
Flame snored in a sunbeam. Pearl bubbled the bath.  
And the cook taught her pancakes, and patience, and math.

## 2. A tingle

Then her silver mark tingled. It fizzed. It went zing!  
Like a tickle that knew about some secret thing.  
Far off, past the garden and over the hill,  
an old pond flashed gold. Then it rumbled. Then: still.

Ruby knew that low rumble. She'd heard it before,  
on her very first night, like a knock at a door.

## 3. The Thunder Egg

Deep down in that pond, where the lily roots grow,  
an egg had been waiting since long, long ago.  
It was yellow and silver, with zigzags of light,  
and it slept through the years, every day, every night.

Now it fizzed. Now it flickered. Now, more than before,  
it was almost ready. Not yet. Just a little bit more.

*Good things hatch when they're ready.*

## 4. Stay home

"Stay here," Ruby told them. "Keep cozy and warm.  
A pond's not for fire, and that sounds like a storm."  
Flame puffed a sad smoke ring. Pearl blew a goodbye.  
Ruby hugged them both twice. Then she gave a brave sigh.

She went on her own, but not quite on her own:  
Pearl's cool, watery bond came along, soft as foam.

## 5. First gets to keep

Across the far hills, in her charcoal-dark tower,  
Noir saw the gold flicker. "A dragon! More power!  
Whoever gets there first gets to keep it," she said.  
War tugged on both boots. (She'd found one under her bed.)  
"Black Hole! Go!" they shouted. And off the three sped.

## 6. Pat-pat, flap-flap

Ruby ran up the hill. Her boots went pat-pat.  
Down the far side came Black Hole: flap-flap, flap-flap,  
with Noir striding after, and War shouting, "GO!"  
They all reached the pond at the same time. Oh no.

## 7. In she goes

"Attack!" shouted War. Black Hole opened his swirl.  
It slurped at the pond with a gulp and a whirl.  
Ruby touched her cool bond. Her dress rippled blue.  
She held up her water hands, took a breath, and dove through.

## 8. Down, down

Down, down, in a bubble her water hands made,  
past minnows and lily stems, into the shade,  
she found it: the Thunder Egg, fizzing and warm.  
She held it so gently. It hummed like a storm.

## 9. Hatch it now!

Above her, the pond sank. Black Hole slurped it in.  
"Crack it!" yelled War. "Make it hatch!" Noir said. "Begin!"  
Ruby wanted to hurry. Her heart thumped like a drum.  
But she'd been an egg too, not so long ago. So she hummed,  
and she held it, and waited, as still as a stone.  
"Take your time," Ruby whispered. "You're not on your own."

*Good things hatch when they're ready.*

## 10. Two!

Then: tap. Then: tap-tap. Then a zig and a zag.  
The shell opened up like a little gift bag.  
Out wiggled two dragons, two sisters, two twins:  
one yellow, with silver eyes, fizzing with grins;  
one silver, with yellow eyes, crackling with cheer.  
"Sparks!" Ruby laughed. "And Crackle! You're here!"

## 11. Zap!

But the swirl pulled the pond, and the pond pulled them too,  
up and up toward the dark, where the slurping wind blew.  
Then Crackle went CRACKLE! And Sparks went ZAP!  
A zigzag of gold shot through water. Snap, snap!  
On Ruby it tingled like fizzy warm rain.  
The twins had already chosen. Their sparks knew her name.

## 12. Hold it!

Up the slurping-up water, the tickly sparks rose,  
up into the swirl, and then up Black Hole's nose.  
His snout started twitching. His eyes squeezed up tight.  
"Hold it!" War shouted. "Black Hole! Hold it tight!"  
"Ah..." said Black Hole. "Ah... ah... AH..."

## 13. ACHOO!

"ACHOO!" Out came the pond, every drop he had drunk,  
in one great big SPLASH! Splish! And splosh! And kerplunk!  
It soaked Noir's tall crown and her long charcoal gown.  
It soaked War from her braid to her boots, all the way down.

## 14. Squelch, squelch

"We're SOGGY!" wailed War. Noir wrung out her sleeve.  
"Home," Noir said, dripping. "Right now. We will leave."  
They squelched down the hill to their kingdom. Achoo!  
And the pond filled back up, sparkly, clear, and brand new.

## 15. One bond for two

Ruby climbed out with a twin tucked in each arm.  
They pressed their small noses to each of her palms.  
Zing! went her mark. And her dress fizzed, then grew  
half yellow, half silver, and crackly too.  
One bond for two sisters who'd hatched side by side.  
Her fingertips sparkled. Her smile opened wide.

## 16. When it's ready

Back home, Flame sniffed Crackle. Pearl bubbled at Sparks.  
Four dragons, one princess, one heap, after dark.  
The big chair still waited, too big and too wide.  
"Someday," Ruby yawned. "When I'm ready inside."

*Good things hatch when they're ready.*
