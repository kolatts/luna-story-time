# Ruby and the Dream Dragon

*Book Seven of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna, Sunny, and Mommy.*

**Refrain:** Dreams are wishes from the heart. (Spreads 2, 10, 14, 16.)

## 1. Sixteen days old

Ruby was sixteen days old, and the night
was velvet and sleepy, the stars twinkling bright.
Eight little dragons curled up on her bed,
and Ruby yawned big as she laid down her head.

"I'll dream of the big chair," she said with a smile.
"I'll dream I'm grown up and I sit there a while."
But Shinebright looked up at the stars, and she sighed,
and wished a small wish that she kept tucked inside.

## 2. A lonely little egg

Far past the stars, where the sleepy dreams play,
a small egg was floating, alone, far away.
Its shell swirled sea-green and blue, sprinkled with pink,
and it glittered and glimmered with every soft blink.

Inside dreamed a dragon named Starry, so small,
so lonely, she wished, "Is there someone at all?
Come find me!" Her wish floated down like a beam,
and it sprinkled on Ruby, just starting to dream.

## 3. Too deep to wake

Ruby fell into sleep that was deep, deep, and deep.
Flame tickled her toes, but she still was asleep.
Lovee kissed her cheek. Sparks and Crackle went zing!
Not a wiggle. Not a peep. Not a stir. Not a thing.

"It's a wish," whispered Shinebright, "that someone made true.
A wish, once begun, must be finished. I'm coming too!"
She curled up on Ruby's soft pillow, so near,
and she shut her bright eyes, and she whispered, "I'm here."

## 4. A bad dream

In the dream, Riverlight was all shadowy gray.
Ruby's dragons curled up, and then, right away,
they were eggs once again: Flame and Pearl, Luna, Leaf,
the twins' Thunder Egg, and Lovee's. Oh, what grief!

Then dream-Noir and dream-War swooped down with a sack.
"They're OURS now!" They scooped every egg. "No take-backs!"
And off to their tower they flew, tall and steep.
Only Shinebright stayed by her side in her sleep.

## 5. Empty hands

Ruby looked at her hands. They were empty and small.
No fire. No bubbles. No sparkles at all.
Her dress was plain plum. Her knees wobbled. She cried,
"I can't do a thing! I've got nothing inside!"

Shinebright nuzzled close. "You still have your heart.
You're still Ruby. And that is the very best part."

## 6. One small step

"It's too much!" Ruby sniffled. "Too many! Too far!"
"Don't do it all," Shinebright said. "Start where you are.
Just take one small step. Then another. Then one."
So Ruby stepped once, and a stone glowed like sun.

She stepped again. Two! And another stone shone.
A path made of steps! She was not on her own.

## 7. The first egg

High up on a gray cloud, an egg glowed bright red.
"Flame!" Ruby climbed, one step, one step ahead.
She hugged the warm egg, and its glow filled the sky,
and the gray turned to lavender, soft as a sigh.

"I'm still scared," Ruby whispered. "My knees still go shake."
"That's okay," said Shinebright. "Brave is the step that you take."

## 8. Smaller and smaller

"Go AWAY!" shrieked dream-Noir from up on her wall.
"You can't!" shouted dream-War. "You're too little and small!"
But Ruby kept stepping. With each step she took,
those two shrank and shrank. Ruby leaned in to look.

They were small as two teacups! "Squeak! Squeak!" went their cries.
"When I face what I'm scared of, it shrinks down in size!"

## 9. Every egg

Pearl's egg in a puddle. Leaf's egg in a tree.
The fizzy-striped Thunder Egg, snug as could be.
Luna's black starry egg on the tower's top stair,
and Lovee's, pink with hearts, hidden under a chair.

One step at a time, Ruby found every one.
Six eggs in her arms! And the gray dream was done.

## 10. One more egg

The gray melted off, and the dream turned to pink,
soft clouds and gold stars, all aglow, all a-wink.
Then Shinebright stopped short. "Ruby, look! Over there!"
One more egg was floating, alone, in the air.

It was swirled green and blue, sprinkled sparkly with pink.
"It looks just like ME," Shinebright whispered. "I think..."

## 11. Awake

Ruby reached out a hand, and she touched the soft shell...
and she BLINKED, and woke up! She was home. All was well.
And her arms were still holding the egg, warm and real!
And all eight of her dragons, not eggs, gave a squeal!

## 12. A bang at the window

Then BANG! went the shutters! In climbed Noir and War,
and Black Hole squeezed after them onto the floor.
"We saw that egg glitter right past our tower!
Hand it over!" Noir snapped. "We want its new power!"

Ruby's knees gave a wobble, the way they did then.
But she thought, "One small step," and she stood up again.

## 13. A sleepy hum

"No," said Ruby, quite calm. And her dragons came too,
in a ring all around her: red, pink, green, and blue.
"Gulp it!" Noir ordered. Black Hole opened wide,
but the egg hummed a lullaby, sleepy inside.

Noir yawned. War yawned. Black Hole yawned. Then all three
were snoring as loud as a snore-y can be!
Pearl bubbled the snorers. Flame warmed it until
it floated them home, far away, over the hill.

## 14. Starry

Then Shinebright curled round the egg, cozy and tight,
and it CRACKED! Out popped a dragon, glittering bright,
with sea-green and blue swirls and a silver tummy below,
but her eyes were bright PINK, with a glittery glow!

"I'm Starry," she peeped. Shinebright gasped, "Sister? YOU?
I wished for a sister each night, the whole night through!"
"And I dreamed of a sister," said Starry, "each night."
So the wish and the dream were the same, shining bright.

## 15. All gold

Then both little sisters, at just the same time,
touched their noses to Ruby's two palms. What a chime!
And EVERYTHING turned GOLD! Gold as gold as could be,
all but Ruby's own face, and the eyes of all three.

Then the gold glow grew soft, and it settled, and stayed:
a dress of bright gold! And in Ruby's hands played
soft swirls of gold dream-dust, as light as a song.
"Nine dragons!" sang Ruby. "Right here we belong!"

## 16. A new dream

That night, nine small dragons curled up on the bed.
"Will you dream of the big chair?" Starry asked. Ruby said,
"I used to. But now I dream something new:
I dream you're all safe and all happy. It's true!"

"Dreams change," Starry whispered, "as you grow, day by day.
A new dream is a good dream. It's okay. It's okay."
Then Ruby yawned big, and nine dragons did too,
and they dreamed the same dream, all the long night through.
