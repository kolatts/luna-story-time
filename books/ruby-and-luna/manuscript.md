# Ruby and Luna

*Book Three of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna, Sunny, and Mommy.*

**Refrain:** *Look for the light, not the color.* Placed on spreads 7, 12, 16.

**Moral:** don't judge a book by its cover, or an egg by its color. War decides the black egg must be bad because it's black, and Noir decides it must be hers because it's "dark just like me." The egg feels their grabby, grumpy hearts and pushes them away, then floats to the kind heart that never asked what color it was. Luna hatches as black as the sky and full of light. For the grown-up: Noir sees in the egg what she sees in herself. We often judge others by our own reflection, and what we grab at tends to slip away.

## Canon and liberties

- A shooting star carries the Starry Egg to Riverlight's palace door on a full-moon night. It is black, speckled with silver stars.
- Ruby is twelve days old.
- Luna is a girl moon dragon: black scales speckled with silver stars, moon-silver eyes. She stays black. Her magic is moonbeams, and her big move is the crashing moon: moonbeams rolled into a soft glowing moon that squashes the bad guys flat (comically, nobody hurt). The real moon in the sky never moves.
- Ruby's Luna bond: a midnight-black dress sprinkled with silver stars, moon-white trim, moonbeams in her hands.
- All four baby dragons save the egg: Pearl's bubble, Flame's warm air (the bubble floats like a balloon), and Sparks and Crackle zipping round Black Hole until he's too dizzy to swirl.
- Black Hole is beaten by a sneeze again: the crashing moon pops into moon-dust that tickles his nose.
- Only Noir and War judge by color. Nobody in Riverlight does.
- Nobody is hurt. Noir, War and Black Hole are sneezed home.

## 1. Full moon

Ruby was twelve days old. She had counted them all.  
The full moon rose up, big and round as a ball.  
Flame toasted a marshmallow. Pearl blew a bubble.  
Sparks and Crackle played chase and got into some trouble.

Then WHOOSH! Something streaked down the sky, long and bright:  
a shooting star, swooping, with sparkles of light.

## 2. Tap, tap

Ruby's silver mark tingled. It fizzed. It went zing!  
The star swooped down lower. It carried a thing.  
It skipped over the river, then hopped the stone wall.  
Then it slowed, and it glowed, and it stopped. That was all.

Tap, tap! came a knock on the big palace door.  
Ruby ran down the hall, and then ran a bit more.

## 3. On the doorstep

On the step sat an egg. It was black, black as night,  
and speckled all over with stars, tiny and bright.  
The shooting star twinkled, then faded away.  
The egg hummed a soft hum, like it wanted to stay.  
Ruby knelt. Her mark fizzed. "Oh," she whispered. "Hello."  
She reached out her hands, oh so gentle, so slow...

## 4. Black is bad?

Across the far hills, in her charcoal-dark tower,  
Noir saw the star fall. "An egg! Dragon power!"  
War squinted. "It's BLACK! Black is spooky! Black's mean!  
It's the baddest, most rotten-est egg ever seen!"  
"Then it's ours," Noir decided. "Black Hole, fly! Be quick!"  
And they swooped through the night with a flap and a flick.

## 5. Attack!

Ruby's fingers were close. Just a little bit more...  
when down swooped Black Hole, right in front of the door.  
"Attack!" shouted War, and she jumped off his back.  
She bumped Ruby, WHUMP, into the cook's laundry stack.

## 6. Mine!

"MINE!" War grabbed. "No, MINE!" Noir grabbed. "Can't you see?  
It's as dark as my tower. It's dark just like me!"  
But the egg felt their hands, grabby, grumpy, and tight,  
and it shivered... then pushed out a ring of starlight.  
BWOMMM! Back slid Noir. Back slid War, on her bum,  
and the egg floated up with a hum, hum, hum.

## 7. The egg chooses

Ruby wriggled out with a sock on her shoe.  
The egg drifted to her, as if it just knew.  
It felt Ruby's heart, gentle, patient, and kind,  
and it glowed, warm and happy. It had made up its mind.  
Then it rang like a bell, a soft call, clear and high,  
and four little dragons looked up at the sky.

*Look for the light, not the color.*

## 8. Slurp!

"Black Hole!" Noir snapped. "Swirl it! Gobble it in!"  
His dark swirl went slurrrp with a whirl and a spin.  
The egg tipped. The egg spun. It slid up through the air,  
toward the swirl! Ruby gasped. "Oh, egg, don't go there!"

## 9. To the rescue

Out raced Flame! Out raced Pearl! Out zoomed Sparks! Crackle, too!  
They had heard the egg calling. They knew what to do.  
Pearl blew a big bubble, bloop! round the egg, snug and sound.  
Flame puffed warm air under it. Up! Off the ground!  
Like a balloon on a breeze, the bubble rose high,  
away from the swirl, to the moon-silver sky.

## 10. Dizzy

Sparks zipped round to the left. Crackle zipped round to the right:  
two zigzagging ribbons of crackly gold light.  
Black Hole's head went around, and around, and around,  
till, dizzy, his swirl fizzled. Pfft! He plopped on the ground.

## 11. Tap, tap, SNAP

Down drifted the bubble, as soft as a sigh,  
into Ruby's two arms, where it popped. Pip! Goodbye!  
The full moon shone down. The egg wiggled. Tap, tap...  
Then a crick, then a crack, then a crickety-SNAP!

## 12. Luna

Out tumbled a dragon, as black as the sky,  
with stars on her scales and two moon-silver eyes.  
She yawned a big yawn, and then WHOOOM! Silver light  
filled the garden, the palace, the hills, and the night!  
"But she's BLACK!" War gasped. "Black things don't shine! Or do they?"  
"Hello, Luna," said Ruby. "You're brighter than day."

*Look for the light, not the color.*

## 13. Moonbeams

Luna nuzzled her nose into Ruby's warm hand.  
Zing! went Ruby's mark, and her dress swished so grand:  
midnight black, sprinkled over with stars shining bright,  
with a trim that glowed softly in moon-silver white.  
And her hands filled with moonbeams, as cool as a stream,  
soft and silver and curling like ribbons in dreams.

## 14. Crashing moon

"That light should be OURS!" Noir snapped. "Black Hole, GO!"  
Black Hole wobbled up, dizzy, his swirl spinning slow.  
Ruby lifted her moonbeams. Luna lifted hers, too.  
They rolled them together, and the moonbeams grew  
into one round, glowing, enormous soft moon...  
and CRASH! Down it came, with a BOING and a BOOM!  
SQUISH! went Noir. SQUASH! went War. SQUOOSH! went Black Hole: flat  
as three pancakes, three pillows, three squashed welcome mats.

## 15. ACHOO!

The squishy moon popped into silvery dust,  
and it tickled Black Hole's nose, as tickly dust must.  
"Ah..." "HOLD IT!" squeaked War, still as flat as a sheet.  
"Ah... ah... ACHOO!" And it blew them right off their feet,  
over hills, past the river, back home to their tower:  
three sneezy, squashed grumps in a moon-dusty shower.

## 16. Look for the light

Back inside, Luna snuggled with Flame, Pearl, and the twins:  
five dragons, one princess, one heap of sleepy grins.  
Luna glowed like a nightlight, all speckled with stars.  
"Some folks see a color," yawned Ruby, "not who you are.  
But you're dark, and you shine. You're both, Luna. It's true."  
And Luna glowed brighter, the way good friends do.

*Look for the light, not the color.*

*The End — of Ruby's third adventure.*
