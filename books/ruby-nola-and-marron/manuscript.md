# Ruby, Nola and Marron

*Book Eight of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna and Sunny.*

**Refrain:** Two can do if they work together. (Spreads 9, 10, 14, 16.)

## 1. Seventeen days old

Ruby was seventeen days old today,
and nine little dragons came tumbling to play.
They rolled down the hill where the buttercups grow,
and they chased their own tails in a wiggly row.

Then Ruby's mark tingled. She stood very still.
Tick... tock... came a sound from beyond the far hill.
"An egg!" Ruby whispered. "It's ready, I know.
But it's over at Noir's. Come, my dragons, let's go!"

## 2. The egg in the clock

In Noir's dark tower, a big clock went TICK.
Behind its round face, where the cobwebs hung thick,
sat an egg made of two eggs, stuck tight, side by side:
one glittery silver, one gold. And inside...

two voices were arguing! "Hurry! Let's GO!"
"No, wait!" said the other. "Not yet! Take it slow!"
Noir peeked in and grinned. "War, come quickly and see!
Two who fight can be split: one for you, one for me!"

## 3. Grizzlemop's stairs

Pearl blew a big bubble, and Ruby climbed in.
Flame puffed it up high with a warm, toasty grin.
They floated past hilltops, the dragons in tow,
to the cave under Noir's, where the tall flowers grow.

"Grizzlemop!" Ruby called. Out he shuffled, all fluff.
"Can you take us up high to the clock?" "Sure enough!"
He led them up stairs that went round, round, and round,
and they tiptoed so softly, they made not a sound.

## 4. Nola and Marron

At the top, gears were turning. A pendulum swung.
And there sat the egg where the big tower bells hung.
"Hurry UP!" said a voice from the silvery side.
"Not YET!" said the gold side. And both of them sighed.

"I'm Nola," said Silver. "I'm time, and I'm FAST!
I can speed things right up! Let's hatch out at last!"
"I'm Marron," said Gold. "I'm for friendship. Let's stay
till we're BOTH good and ready. Then hatch the same day!"

## 5. Noir's whispers

Then in swept Noir and War, with Black Hole trailing slow.
Noir knelt by the egg, and she whispered down low,
"Nola, dear, she just slows you. Fly off on your own!"
"Marron, dear, she's so bossy. You'd be better alone!"

War grinned, "I want GOLD!" Black Hole stared at the floor.
He remembered a rose and a bench from before.
"Don't listen!" cried Ruby. But Nola said, "NOW!
I'm hatching this minute! I'll show you just how!"

## 6. Half hatched

She twirled up her magic, all silver and bright,
and she sped up the hatching with all of her might.
CRACK! Out popped a silver head, one wing, one leg...
but Marron's gold half was still stuck in the egg!

She flapped her one wing, and she tipped to one side,
and she spun round and round. "I can't steer!" Nola cried.
From inside the gold shell, Marron called, soft and small,
"Half a dragon can't fly. Not alone. Not at all."

## 7. Time goes wild

And her magic ran wild! All the clock hands went WHIRR!
Every gear spun so fast that it turned to a blur.
The sun zipped up, down, and the moon zipped by fast.
Flowers bloomed, drooped, and bloomed again, zooming past.

War's braid grew so long that it piled on the floor.
Black Hole's tail did a twirl, and then twelve twirls more!
"I can't stop it!" cried Nola. "It's too big for me!"
And the tall tower went wibble-wob, wobbly-wheee!

## 8. Split them up

"Now's our chance!" shouted Noir, and she held out her sack.
"Come to me, speedy Nola, and never look back!"
War hugged the gold shell. "This half's MINE! Hear me? MINE!"
Inside, little Marron let out a sad whine.

Ruby stepped in between, and she knelt on the floor.
"Nola, why are you hurrying? What is it for?
And Marron, why wait?" Ruby asked, soft and kind.
"Tell me both what you want. Tell me what's on your mind."

## 9. The same wish

"I wanted to see the whole world," Nola said,
"with Marron right next to me, head next to head!"
"And I," whispered Marron, "just wanted to wait,
so we'd see it TOGETHER. I don't mind being late."

Ruby smiled. "Then you both want the very same thing!
Fast and patient together can do anything.
Like the sun and the moon, like the day and the night,
you are different, and that's what can make you just right."

## 10. Hold on together

"Everybody hold on!" Ruby called. And they came:
Pearl and Luna and Leaf, Sparks and Crackle and Flame,
Shinebright, Starry, and Lovee, and big Grizzlemop too,
paw in paw, tail in tail, in a ring that held true.

Then Marron glowed gold, and the gold glow spread wide.
Every friend felt stronger with friends by their side.
"Now, Nola," said Marron, "let's do it as two."
"Together," said Nola. "I'm ready. Are you?"

## 11. Just the right speed

Nola sped up her magic, but gently this time:
just the shell, just a little, as soft as a chime.
Crick... crack... and out tumbled a gold head and wing,
and a gold leg and paw! Then a hug! What a thing!

Then she gathered her magic back in, strand by strand,
like a kite on a string floating home to her hand.
The sun settled. The gears slowed. The clock said, "Tick... tock."
But War's braid stayed so long, it reached all the way round the block!

## 12. Two heads, one bond

Two heads gave a nuzzle. One tail curled up tight.
"We hatched out TOGETHER!" they cheered with delight.
Then the silver nose pressed on Ruby's left hand,
and the gold nose pressed right. Oh, the feeling was grand!

Her dress shimmered half silver, half gold, bright and new,
sprinkled with hourglasses and hands holding hands, too!
Silver time-sparkles danced in her left hand, so light,
and her right hand glowed gold. "Ten dragons! What a sight!"

## 13. Black Hole's choice

"Black Hole!" shouted Noir. "GULP that dragon up, quick!"
Black Hole opened his swirl. Then he shut it. Click-click.
He looked at the ring, every paw holding tight,
and one empty space, just his size, in the light.

Then Marron's gold glow brushed his nose like a kiss.
"I have never," he whispered, "felt something like this."
"You're OUR dragon!" War shouted. Black Hole shook his head.
"A dragon gets to choose who he's with," he said.

## 14. Holding on

He padded to Ruby and lowered his head,
and he pressed his nose soft to her palm instead.
"I'm all done with grabbing," he rumbled. "It's true.
I would rather hold on. Can I hold on with you?"

Then her dress turned to charcoal with lavender shine,
with a violet hem twirling round, oh so fine!
and in her two palms spun small swirls, soft and round.
"Eleven!" laughed Ruby. "Look what we have found!"

## 15. Stomping home

Noir stamped and War stomped down the stairs, round and round,
with no egg and no dragon, not one dragon found.
War's long braid went bump-bumping each step of the way,
and the palace felt quiet and empty and gray.

Ruby's bubble rose high, and she waved from the sky.
War's hand started to wave... then she turned with a sigh.
"Someday," Ruby whispered, "I hope that they'll find
there's room on a team for a heart that is kind."

## 16. Home together

Back home in Riverlight, at the end of the day,
eleven good dragons were tired from play.
"Let's race!" Nola said. "Let's stroll," Marron said.
So they strolled, then they raced, all the way up to bed!

Black Hole curled at the foot of the bed, warm and wide,
and Ruby kissed heads, every one, side by side:
twelve heads on eleven good dragons! Then, deep
in one great big cuddle, they all fell asleep.
