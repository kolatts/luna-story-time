# Ruby and the Leafy Egg

*Book Four of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna, Sunny, and Mommy.*

**Refrain:** *Give a hand, and give again.* Placed on spreads 8, 13, 16.

**Moral:** be generous, and help others even when they make mistakes. The cave monster roars and knocks off Ruby's crown, and she frees him anyway. Noir and War caused the whole mess by trying to grab the egg, and Ruby and her dragons rebuild their castle anyway, with no thank-you. For the grown-up: a gift that waits for a thank-you is really a trade. Kindness given to people who got it wrong (and who may never say so) is the kind that costs something, and the kind that is most free.

## Canon and liberties

- Ruby is thirteen days old.
- The Leafy Egg is buried deep in a cave under Noir and War's charcoal-and-violet palace. Noir knows it is there and orders Black Hole to gulp his way down through the cellar stones to reach it. His gulping shakes the hill, the frightened egg shivers back (an earth egg's shiver shakes the ground), and the castle crumbles.
- The five dragons feel the rumble through their toes and lead Ruby to the cave. Ruby's mark tingles at the cave mouth.
- Ruby goes into the cave with all five dragons. Inside is Grizzlemop, a great big hairy cave monster, roaring because a rock from the shaking pinned his tail. He swats Ruby's crown off; she frees him anyway; he says sorry, gives back her crown, and shows them the egg.
- Leaf is a girl earth dragon who looks like a leaf: green, leaf-veined, petal-pink eyes. When she hatches, the shaking stops.
- Ruby's Leaf bond: a spring-green dress with a hem of pink petals; tiny flowering green vines curl from her fingertips.
- Leaf's big move: flowers and vines that hold stones together.
- Black Hole sneezes again, but this time the sneeze puts the castle back: Leaf's flower dust tickles his nose and he sneezes out every stone he swallowed. Leaf's flowers hold them in place.
- Noir and War do not change and do not say thank you. Ruby helps anyway.

---

## 1. Rumble

Ruby was thirteen days old. She had counted each one.  
She was stacking up blocks in the warm morning sun.  
Then the teacups went rattle. The windows went hum.  
And the floor gave a rumble, like a big, bumpy drum.

RUMBLE, RUMBLE! Her block tower tumbled and fell.  
Something deep in the ground had a story to tell.

## 2. Gulp!

Across the far hills, in her charcoal-dark tower,  
Noir rubbed her hands. "There's an egg! Dragon power!  
It's buried down deep, underneath our own floor.  
Black Hole, gulp the stones! Gulp them up! Gulp some more!"

GULP! went the cellar. GLUG! went a wall.  
GULP! went the pillars that held up the hall.  
Every gulp made a rumble. And deep down below,  
a scared little egg shivered. The whole hill shook so!

## 3. Crumble

The tower went wibble. The walls went a-wobble.  
Down tumbled the bricks with a bump and a bobble.  
Noir held up a doorway. War pushed on a wall.  
"Stop GULPING!" they shouted. "You'll tumble it all!"  
Black Hole stopped, round and stuffed, with a BURP. But below,  
the scared egg kept on shivering. The hill shook. Oh no!

## 4. Follow the rumble

The five little dragons pressed their toes to the ground.  
They could feel it: a tremble, a hum, a deep sound.  
Flame tiptoed. Pearl tiptoed. The twins went zip-zip.  
Luna padded ahead with her nose to each dip.  
Over meadows and creeks, over hill after hill,  
they followed the rumble, and it rumbled on still.

## 5. The cave

Under Noir's crumbly castle, a cave mouth yawned wide.  
Ruby's silver mark tingled. Zing! "There's an egg inside!"  
"GO AWAY!" yelled War from the top of a wall.  
"That egg is OURS!" Noir snapped. "We found it! That's all!"  
"Somebody's frightened," Ruby whispered, "down there."  
She held close to her dragons, and stepped in with care.

## 6. Drip, drop

It was dark in the cave. Drip! Drop! went the drips.  
Long roots dangled down like old wiggly fingertips.  
Luna glowed soft and silver. Flame puffed a warm light.  
Echoes whispered "...ello... ...ello..." left and right.  
"I'm scared," Ruby whispered. "But I'll go anyway."  
And her dragons stayed close, every step of the way.

## 7. ROAR!

Then the dark gave a huff. And a puff. And a THUMP.  
And out loomed a monster, a great, hairy lump!  
He was big as a haystack, all shaggy and brown,  
with two glowing eyes, and a growl, and a frown.  
"ROOOAAAR!" went the monster, so loud that rocks rolled,  
and he swatted Ruby's crown off. Clink! went the gold.

## 8. Stuck

The dragons hid, trembling, tails tucked in tight.  
But Ruby looked closer, by Luna's soft light.  
Then she saw why he roared. From the shaking, a rock  
had dropped on his tail, and it held like a lock.  
"He knocked off my crown, and that wasn't okay.  
But he's hurting," said Ruby. "Let's help anyway."

*Give a hand, and give again.*

## 9. Heave-ho!

"Heave!" pushed Ruby. "Ho!" pushed five dragons. Then... ROLL!  
Off tumbled the rock with a bump down a hole.  
The monster sat up. He gave back her crown with a sigh.  
"I'm Grizzlemop. Sorry I roared," he said, shy.  
"You helped me, though I was so grumpy and rough.  
The egg is this way. It's been scared long enough."

## 10. The leafy egg

Down, down, through a tunnel, past roots, round a bend,  
was a cave full of green at the tunnel's far end.  
And there, in a nest made of moss, soft and deep,  
sat an egg wearing leaves, much too scared to sleep.  
It shivered. It shook. RUMBLE, RUMBLE went the ground.  
Ruby hugged it up close. "Shh. You're safe now. You're found."

## 11. Crick, crack, SNAP

She hummed a soft hum, and she rocked it just so,  
till its shivers grew smaller, and slower, and slow...  
Then a crick! Then a crack! Then a crinkly-leaf SNAP!  
Out tumbled a dragon, right into her lap.  
She was green as a leaf, with two petal-pink eyes.  
And the shaking just STOPPED. What a quiet surprise!

## 12. Leaf

"Hello, Leaf," Ruby whispered. Leaf gave a green grin,  
and she pressed her small nose to Ruby's palm. Zing!  
Ruby's dress swirled spring green, as fresh as new leaves,  
with a hem of pink petals that swirled like a breeze,  
and from her fingertips, little vines curled and grew,  
with wee flowers that opened, pink, yellow, and blue.

## 13. The heap

Up, up, out they climbed, to the top of the hill.  
Noir's castle lay crumbled, all dusty and still.  
"That's OUR egg!" Noir snapped. "Hand that dragon to me!"  
But Leaf hopped to Ruby, as snug as could be.  
Noir and War made this mess. They were grabby, it's true.  
Ruby looked at the rubble... and knew what to do.

*Give a hand, and give again.*

## 14. ACHOO!

"Black Hole swallowed your stones," Ruby said. "They're in there."  
Leaf blew a gold puff of flower dust into the air.  
It tickled Black Hole's nose. "Ah..." "Don't!" War said. "Ah... ah..."  
"ACHOOOO!" Out they flew! Stones and pillars! Ta-da!  
Back up flew the bricks and the stairs, block by block,  
to the walls and the tower. Clink! Clunk! Clonk! Clock!

## 15. Flowers hold tight

But the stones wibbled. Wobbled. "Oh, Leaf," Ruby said,  
"can we help them hold tight?" And Leaf nodded her head.  
Up sprang vines! Up sprang flowers, in every last crack,  
holding stone onto stone, side to side, front to back.  
Grizzlemop carried big stones. Pearl washed off the dust.  
Flame, the twins, and Luna glowed, as good helpers must.

## 16. Give again

The castle stood tall, wrapped in flowers, brand new.  
Noir sniffed. "Pink flowers? Not OUR color. Pooh."  
War crossed her arms. "And no thank-yous from me!"  
"That's okay," Ruby smiled. "A gift's given free."  
Grizzlemop waved from his cave as the sun dipped down low,  
and home went six dragons, one princess, all aglow.

*Give a hand, and give again.*

*The End — of Ruby's fourth adventure.*
