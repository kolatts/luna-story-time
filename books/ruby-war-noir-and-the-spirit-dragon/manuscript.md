# Ruby, War, Noir, and the Spirit Dragon

*Book Nine of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna and Sunny.*

**Refrain:** It's never too late to be kind. (Spreads 8, 11, 14, 16.)

## 1. Eighteen days old

Ruby was eighteen days old. In the sun,
eleven good dragons came out for some fun.
"Everybody hold on!" Ruby called. So they stood
paw in paw, tail in tail, and oh, it felt good!

Then her mark didn't tingle. It GLOWED, soft and warm,
and up from the ring swirled a glittery storm:
fire and bubbles and moonbeams and vines,
sparkles and hearts in bright, wiggly lines!

## 2. The egg nobody found

The lights twirled together, up high in the air,
and POP! In the middle, an egg was just THERE!
Not found, and not taken, and nobody's prize.
It was made out of holding on. What a surprise!

It shone every color, like fireworks at night,
and inside, someone giggled, "Hee hee!" with delight.
One giggle puffed fire. The next, bubbles flew!
"A spirit egg," Shinebright said, "made from all of you!"

## 3. Across the far hills

Far over the hills, in a palace of gray,
sat Noir and sat War, with no dragon to play.
"That Ruby is CRUEL!" grumbled War. "It's not fair!
She took EVERY dragon, and not one did she share!"

"She took our Black Hole, too," sniffed Noir. "And WE
were so NICE! We kept eggs in a chest, under key!"
Then a rainbow light flashed past the hills, far away.
"It's those BONDS of hers, War! We shall break them today!"

## 4. You are cruel!

They walked the whole way, with no dragon to fly,
over hill after hill, as the morning went by,
and War's long, long braid dragged behind in the dew.
Then they stomped through the gate. "Ruby! We've come for YOU!"

"You are CRUEL!" shouted War. "You took dragons galore!"
"You took our Black Hole!" Noir said. "And you'll take more!"
Ruby blinked. "ME? Cruel?" And she thought it all through.
"Dragons, DID I take you? Tell me true. Tell me true."

## 5. Pull!

"You never once took us!" said Shinebright. "We chose!"
And every head nodded, from horns down to nose.
"Those bonds MAKE them say it!" snapped Noir. "We shall break them!
And once they are broken, we'll free them... and take them!"

War looped her long braid round the ring like a rope.
They heaved and they hauled up the grassy green slope,
till paws slipped from paws, and tails slid out of tails,
and the ring came apart with eleven small wails.

## 6. A bond is not a rope

But the bonds didn't break. Not a one. Not a thread.
"A bond's not a rope you can pull," Ruby said.
"It's a choosing. It's here." And she tapped on her heart.
"You can't break it by pulling two friends far apart."

But the egg! It stopped giggling. It pulled in its glow,
and its shell shut up tight as a nut. "Oh no! No!"
Flame warmed it. Pearl hummed. Nola tried a quick spell.
"It won't hatch near grabbing," said Shinebright. "I can tell."

## 7. A hard thing to say

"It's BROKEN!" said Noir. "Hand it here. We'll take care.
We've a nice iron chest. We will keep it safe there."
Then somebody big padded in, soft and slow,
and stood right in front of them. Black Hole said, "No."

"I was your dragon," he rumbled, "and so
I know you. And there's something you two need to know.
It's a hard thing to hear. It is hard, and it's true.
So I'll say it as kindly as I can to you."

## 8. The true thing

"Ruby never took dragons. Not me. Not a one.
She held out her hand. We could stay. We could run.
She asked what we wanted. She listened. She heard.
You never once asked me. Not one single word."

"That chest wasn't safe. It was dark. Pearl was scared.
You said, 'Gulp it!' and 'Grab it!' and nobody cared
how it felt for the eggs, or for me. That's unkind.
It was cruel. I was, too. But here's what I find:"

## 9. Who is afraid?

Noir opened her mouth, but no word tumbled out.
War looked at the dragons. And what were they about?
They were hiding from HER, behind Ruby, in a row,
peeking out with big eyes, and all trembling so.

"They're scared," whispered War. "Are they scared... of ME?
But I thought we were nice! And the cruel one was she!"
Noir looked at her sack, and she thought of the chest.
"All this time," she said softly, "we thought we knew best."

## 10. Sorry

War let go of her braid. Then she said, very small,
"I'm sorry for being so cruel to you all.
I grabbed and I shouted. I never asked you.
And Ruby, I called YOU the cruel one. Not true."

Noir knelt in the grass, and she bowed her dark head.
"I'm sorry for being unkind," Noir said.
"For the chest, and the sacks, and each 'Gulp it!' and 'MINE!'
And Black Hole, I bossed you about all the time."

## 11. Forgiven

It was quiet. Then Pearl, who'd been locked in that chest,
tiptoed out, and she nuzzled Noir's hand. Then the rest
crept out, one by one, from the place where they hid.
"I forgive you," said Ruby. And she truly did.

"Saying sorry is brave. It was hard, and you tried.
Now you get to start over. Come sit by my side."
Nobody was grabbing. Not a hand. Not a paw.
And the egg gave a wiggle... a "hee!"... and a "haw!"

## 12. Giggles

CRACK! went the egg, with a rainbow KA-BOOM!
Fireworks fizzed up like flowers in bloom.
Out tumbled a dragon, all rainbow, all bright,
from her little red nose to her tail's silver light.

She had Flame's orange frills, and a heart on her chest
just like Lovee's, and Leaf's leafy collar. The rest?
One horn gold, one horn silver, and violet wings, too!
"I'm Giggles!" she giggled. "I'm a bit of all of you!"

## 13. Every dress in one

Giggles pressed her warm nose into Ruby's palm. POP!
And her dress changed its colors from bottom to top:
a patch of each dress she had worn up till now,
all stitched into one! And the dragons said, "WOW!"

Ember red, river blue, starry black, leafy green,
pink with hearts, gold and silver, with violet between,
and bright rainbow fireworks on every last one.
In her hands, tiny fireworks fizzed, just for fun!

## 14. A spark for each

Then Giggles looked over at Noir and at War,
who stood at the edge. "Hee hee! There is more!"
She giggled a giggle that nobody knew,
and out floated two rainbow sparks, just for two.

They landed, as soft as a kiss, on each heart.
"They will glow when you're kind. That's a light for your start."
"Can we be your friends?" War asked, quiet and slow.
"YES!" Ruby laughed. "There was always room. Now you know."

## 15. Big enough

That night, Giggles giggled her very best "HEE!"
and rainbow fireworks went BOOM! One, two, three!
Big Grizzlemop carried the big chair outside,
and War's braid made a jump rope, a whole dragon wide!

Then onto the big chair they piled, with a cheer:
twelve dragons, and Ruby, with War and Noir near.
"It was never too big," Ruby said with a grin.
"It was just big enough to fit everyone in!"

## 16. One great big cuddle

When the last sparkle faded, they yawned, every one.
"Please stay," Ruby said. "We have only begun."
Noir tucked a soft blanket round each sleepy head:
thirteen heads on twelve dragons, two girls, in one bed!

Two heart-lights shone warm. And far off, through the night,
their gray palace bloomed every color, all bright.
Then, snuggled together, all cozy and deep,
in one great big cuddle, they all fell asleep.
