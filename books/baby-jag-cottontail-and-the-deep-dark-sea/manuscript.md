# Baby Jag Cottontail and the Deep Dark Sea

*Book Two of the Castle Everstair stories*

---

## Liberties I took

You said decide, so here they are in one place — easy to overrule:

- **The title is *Baby Jag Cottontail and the Deep Dark Sea*** — her name on the cover of her own debut.
- **The theft is stolen reflections.** Beedlist gathers the girls' reflections off the water; wherever your reflection goes, your powers follow. They watch it happen and cannot stop it — you can't keep the sea from holding your face.
- **Beedlist is the still one.** She never speaks in the whole book. She never hurries. Even Evilest whispers around her. She is not more of Evilest — she is deep where Evilest is loud.
- **Cottontail arrives by driftwood log** from "an island of somewheres," wears a woven seashell bracelet, and her claws are ordinary claws — that's the point.
- **The refrain:** *"But the deep can't keep what was always yours."* It appears four times — early, mid, at the turn, at the close — and the last time it appears, it is no longer entirely comforting.
- **The cliffhanger:** the powers come home, the table is warm — and on the last page Moon's reflection waves back *a moment too late*. Something in the deep kept a copy. Nothing is resolved.
- **The fifth bowl** — soup for four friends, and one bowl set out anyway, for anyone stormy who might not come.
- **Beedlist's book** — on spread 14 she writes something in a small black book with no name on it. This is Book Three's door, left open.

## The manuscript — 16 spreads

### Spread 1 — The castle sea

The stair goes up. You know about the stair.
But turn around backward, and look — over there.
The sea starts right where the bottom step ends,
and it goes out forever, and never pretends.

And cupped in the rocks, where the seafoam lies flat,
there's a pool, round and warm as a windowsill cat,
where the sea comes to rest when it's done being wide.
The pool is the sea with the wildness outside.

### Spread 2 — Summer of three

All summer they floated: a girl and a pup,
tails down in the water and faces tipped up,
and Cheeblest beside them, with bows in her hair,
learning that summer is something you share.

But sometimes she'd look past the waves, past the spray,
at a tower she used to live in, far away,
and her face would go quiet, the way faces do
when the old thing still lives in the middle of you.

### Spread 3 — The visitor

Then one bright blue morning the sea brought a guest:
a jaguar — gold, with black spots on her chest,
with pink hair blown sideways from riding the gale
on a driftwood-log boat with a green-leaf sail.

She hopped off and bowed, with her bracelet of shell.
"I'm Baby Jag Cottontail. I sail. And I'm well."

### Spread 4 — Zero

"How old are you two?" she asked. "Zero," said Moon.
"We were born when the moon was. We're new as the moon."
"Well," said Cottontail, "I'm older than that.
I know things you don't. I'm that kind of cat.

I know which waves bite. I know where it's deep.
I know how to sing a rough sea back to sleep."
And she stretched out her paw, and her claws, small and bare:
"No magic," she said. "Just the things that I wear."

### Spread 5 — The far shore

But across the wide water, on wet black sand,
the tower had come down to the sea to stand.
Evilest, crowned, was hollering things.
The water near *her* made rages and rings —

but beside her stood somebody new. Somebody small.
All black. And all quiet. And no crown at all.
Beedlist. The still one. As deep as the deep.
The sea around Beedlist lay flat as sleep.

And Cheeblest went cold, though the day was still warm:
"They're coming. I know them. And this time's a new form."

### Spread 6 — The gathering

They came in the morning. They stood on their shore.
No lantern. No present. No trick at the door.
Beedlist knelt down and looked into the sea,
and the sea started giving up things that aren't free:

reflections. The girls watched their own faces rise
up out of the water, to their surprise,
and walk on the waves to the black sister's hands.
You can't stop the water from holding your glance.

*But the deep can't keep what was always yours.*

### Spread 7 — The following

For wherever your reflection goes, powers go too.
That's a rule of the water. It's old, and it's true.
First swimming-like-silver. Then turning-time-gold.
Then warm went away, and the pool went cold.

Moon reached for her fire and caught only air.
It had followed her face. It was over there.

### Spread 8 — The gift

Cottontail said, "Give the rest to me.
My face is my own. It's not in their sea.
They never once gathered a copy of mine.
I'll carry your shine across the brine."

And here's what is heavy, and here's what is true:
they gave her *everything*. Somebody new.
A friend of one morning, one swim, one shore —
they poured out their pockets, and gave, and gave more.

### Spread 9 — The failing

She ran on the water with borrowed-bright feet,
glowing like lighthouses, hard to unseat.
But out on the waves your face shows below,
and Beedlist just gathered it, gentle and slow.

The powers slid off her like rain off a leaf,
and the deep dark sea pulled her down underneath.

*But the deep can't keep what was always yours.*

### Spread 10 — The deep dark sea

Down is a country with nothing to see.
Down went the jaguar. Down went she.
No fire to warm her. No time to turn gold.
No borrowed shine left her. Just dark. Just cold.

And there at the bottom of all of the blue,
she opened her paw. And her claws came through.
Not given. Not taken. Not shiny. Not new.
Just hers. They had grown there. They'd grown as she grew.

### Spread 11 — The girl in the middle

On the shore stood Cheeblest, one heart, two lands:
her sisters behind her with hands full of strands,
her friends' empty pool on the opposite side,
and nothing to stand on between them but tide.

And she shouted the thing that a little one knows,
loud enough that it rattled her bows:
"You can hold every face in the sea in your fist —
it still won't be *you* that the faces have missed!"

### Spread 12 — Claws

Claws are not magic. Claws are just claws.
They don't follow reflections. They don't follow laws.
They gripped the wet rock that no power could hold,
and up climbed the jaguar, out of the cold.

At the top sat the basin of gathered-up glass,
of faces and firelight, quiet as grass.
One paw. One claw. One thin little line —
and the dark shell split open, and out poured the shine.

*But the deep can't keep what was always yours.*

### Spread 13 — Home across the water

Reflections ran home over waves, like light,
each face to its owner, each shine to its right.
The pool filled back up with two faces it knew,
and the sea went from grey into ordinary blue.

And high on the rock stood a soggy young cat,
who had saved them with nothing but claws. Only that.
(Only that! says the sea. As if *that* were small.
The thing that was hers was the biggest of all.)

### Spread 14 — The still one

Evilest yelled fit to crumble the shore.
(She'd been mad once. Never this mad before.)
But Beedlist stood still in her black, in her quiet,
and looked at the jaguar. Just looked. Didn't try it.

Then Beedlist did something she'd never once done:
she smiled — very small, like a wintertime sun —
and she wrote something down in a book with no name.
And the sea, for a moment, went still as a frame.

### Spread 15 — Soup, again

They paddled home slow, and they dripped up the stair,
and they set out the table with room and to spare:
four bowls for four friends, all steaming and bright,
and a fifth bowl set extra, in front of a chair,
for anyone stormy who might not be there.

The powers trickled home the same way as before —
slowly, and mostly. Moon minded it less than before.
And Cottontail yawned, and she didn't sail on.
Some friends, once they find you, are never quite gone.

### Spread 16 — The pool, after

Later that night, when the lamps were all low,
Moon went to the pool, just to tell it hello.
Her face looked back up at her, pink hair and crown,
the moon up above, and the moon looking down.

She waved at herself, like you do, like you do.

And her reflection waved back.

A moment too late.

*But the deep can't keep what was always yours.*

**The End** — *of the second one.*

## Art direction

### Global style string
> Painterly children's storybook illustration, soft watercolor and colored-pencil texture, visible brushwork, warm golden lamplight against deep indigo night, lavender and pearl-grey stone, generous sparkle and stardust trails, big expressive eyes, gentle rounded shapes, no harsh lines, no photorealism, no text in image.

### Character sheet (repeat verbatim in every prompt)
- **Princess Moon** — young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant.
- **Baby Lady** — small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
- **Baby Jag Cottontail** — young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg.
- **Evilest** — pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile.
- **Cheeblest** — pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower.
- **Beedlist** — pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her.
- **Recurring motif** — the crescent moon, plus (new this book) small seashells tucked into scenes.

### Per-spread prompts

1. Wide horizontal seascape at night: a lavender fairy-tale castle on cliffs at the right edge with the bottom steps of a grand stair meeting the water, a vast calm indigo sea stretching left to the horizon, a small round rock pool glowing warm in the foreground, full moon low over the water. No characters. Calm, wide, deep.
2. The rock pool in golden evening light: Moon and Baby Lady floating happily, tails in the water, faces tipped up; Cheeblest sitting on the pool's stone rim with her feet in, laughing — but her eyes drifting toward a tiny dark tower far across the sea.
3. Bright blue morning: Cottontail arriving on a driftwood-log boat with a green-leaf sail, hopping ashore mid-bow, pink hair windblown; Moon, Baby Lady and Cheeblest at the pool's edge looking up delighted.
4. Warm close-up at the moonlit pool edge: Cottontail gently holding up one paw showing small ordinary claws; Moon and Baby Lady leaning in fascinated; friendship, curiosity, no fear.
5. The far shore, wet black sand: Evilest mid-holler, fists up, water making angry rings around her — and beside her Beedlist standing utterly still, hands folded, the sea around Beedlist flat as glass; grim tower behind them.
6. Beedlist kneeling at the water's mirror-calm edge as glowing translucent reflections of Moon and Baby Lady rise off the water's surface and walk across the sea toward her folded hands; far across the water the real Moon and Baby Lady hold each other at the pool, watching. Eerie but gentle.
7. The pool gone grey and cold: Moon reaching out over the water, her open hand making no fire, only air; Baby Lady drooping beside her; the water where their reflections should be showing only empty sky. Quiet loss.
8. The gift, solemn and beautiful: Moon and Baby Lady pouring long streams of gold, silver and pink light from their hands and paws into Cottontail, who begins to glow all over; night pool, hushed faces, weight and trust.
9. Cottontail running across the wave-tops glowing with borrowed light, mid-stride, light streaming off her like rain — far ahead on the shore, a small perfectly still black figure kneels at the water; the glow guttering at Cottontail's paws.
10. Deep underwater, blue-black and vast: Cottontail sinking small through the dark with tiny bubbles, one thin shaft of moonlight reaching her — her eyes open, calm, looking at her own outstretched paw where small claws catch the light.
11. Cheeblest alone on a narrow stone causeway between two shores, wind pulling her bows sideways, small fists clenched, shouting bravely across the water; the dark tower far on one side, the warm pool-glow far on the other. The middle of everything.
12. Cottontail climbing a sheer wet black rock face out of the crashing sea, claws dug in, soaked and determined; above her at the summit a wide black basin glinting with gathered faces and firelight.
13. Triumphant flood of light: the black basin split by one thin bright scratch, reflections pouring out as ribbons of light streaming home in every direction across the night sea; Cottontail silhouetted small and proud against the flood.
14. Night shore: Evilest raging comically, crown askew, fists balled — while Beedlist stands perfectly still all in black beside her with a very small calm smile, writing in a small black book; the sea behind them unnaturally flat.
15. Cozy interior at night: the round wooden table under lamplight with five bowls of steaming soup — Moon, Baby Lady, Cheeblest and Cottontail seated warm and tired, and one empty chair with a full bowl waiting in front of it; crescent moon carved above the door.
16. Quiet night close-up at the pool: Moon leaning over the still water saying goodnight, the full moon overhead — and in the water her reflection looks up with one arm raised in a wave, while the real Moon's arms are lowered. Beautiful, hushed, just slightly wrong.

**Cover:** Baby Jag Cottontail front and center riding her driftwood-log boat over a moonlit wave, pink hair flying, seashell bracelet catching the light — Moon flying and Baby Lady swimming joyfully alongside — the lavender castle small and far behind, the sea below deep and dark. Open sky in the upper third for the title.
