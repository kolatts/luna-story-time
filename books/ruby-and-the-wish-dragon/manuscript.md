# Ruby and the Wish Dragon

*Book Five of Ruby and the Dragon Bonds*
*Dreamed up by Luna. Written with love by Luna and Sunny.*

**Refrain:** *Be careful what you wish for. Wishes come true!* Placed on spreads 5, 11, 14 and 16.

**Moral:** be careful what you wish for. Noir tricks Ruby into starting a wish and shouts the ending: that ALL the dragons were theirs. It comes true, and every dragon in the world crowds their palace, but not one will play, snuggle or shine for them. For the grown-up: getting everything you grab for doesn't fill you up, and love can be chosen but never owned. Ruby's own wish lets go of the outcome: she wishes the dragons free to choose, even if they don't choose her.

## Canon and liberties

- Ruby is fourteen days old. She has six dragons at the start (Flame, Pearl, Sparks and Crackle, Luna, Leaf).
- The Rainbow Egg sits at the end of a rainbow in the meadow after rain. Ruby's mark tingles.
- Shinebright is a girl wish dragon: green-blue scales and paws; glittery silver belly, neck and tail undersides, wings and paw pads; glittery silver-and-gold spikes. She bonds with Ruby at hatching. Wish dragons talk.
- Ruby's Shinebright bond: swirled green-and-blue dress with a glittery silver sash and hem; gold-and-silver glitter at her fingertips.
- A wish dragon hatches holding two wishes: one to give, one kept in case a wish goes wrong. A wish, once begun, must be finished.
- Noir's trick: she gets Ruby to say "I wish..." and shouts the ending herself. Every dragon anywhere (including Shinebright) goes to Noir's palace. Bonds still tug in Ruby's heart.
- Backfire: the dragons are theirs but won't play, snuggle or shine. Noir and War's feelings are hurt; nobody is physically hurt.
- Black Hole sneezes when a fluffy feathery dragon tail tickles his nose. The sneeze shakes loose Shinebright's spare wish, which floats to Ruby.
- Ruby's wish: every dragon can choose where to be. Wild dragons go home; all seven of Ruby's choose her.
- Noir and War end up arguing over whose fault it was. No change of heart.

## 1. The rainbow

Ruby was fourteen days old, and the rain had just stopped.  
She went splashing through puddles. Plip! Plop! Splish! Splop!  
Her six little dragons came splashing in too,  
when a rainbow swooped down, red and yellow and blue.  

It curved over the meadow and touched down so near,  
and Ruby's mark tingled. Zing! "There's an egg here!"  

## 2. The rainbow egg

At the end of the rainbow, tucked deep in the grass,  
sat an egg that shone bright as a window of glass.  
It was swirly with green. It was swirly with blue,  
with a glitter of silver, and gold sparkles too.  

Ruby picked it up gently. It hummed a sweet hum.  
"Hello, little egg. Are you ready to come?"  

## 3. Tink, tink, POP

Then a tink! Then a tink! Then a glittery POP!  
Out climbed a small dragon, with a wiggle and hop.  
Her back and her paws were all green-blue, like the sea.  
But her underneath sparkled as bright as could be:  

silver belly, silver neck, silver wings, silver tail,  
silver under her paws, like a glittery trail.  
And down her back marched little spikes in a row,  
gold and silver and glittery, all aglow.  

## 4. Hello, Shinebright

"Hello, Shinebright," said Ruby. Shinebright gave a bright grin,  
and she pressed her small nose to Ruby's palm. Zing!  

Ruby's dress swirled to green and to blue, like the sea,  
with a sash of bright silver, as sparkly as she.  
And her fingertips fizzed with a gold-silver light,  
little glittery sparkles that twinkled so bright.  

## 5. Two wishes

Then Shinebright spoke up, like a tiny bell's chime:  
"I'm a wish dragon, Ruby, so listen. It's time.  
I hatch holding two wishes. The first is for you.  
The other I keep, if a wish goes askew.  

Wishes are big, and they do what they say."  
Ruby thought very hard. "What's a good wish today?"  

*Be careful what you wish for. Wishes come true!*

## 6. A sweet smile

Then a shadow swooped low, with a whoosh and a thrill:  
Black Hole, carrying Noir and War over the hill!  
Noir smiled a sweet smile (Noir is never that sweet).  
"Oh, a WISH dragon! Darling, how lovely! How neat!  

Every wish needs its words. Say them loud, say them clear.  
Start with 'I wish,' darling. We're all listening here."  

## 7. The trick

Ruby didn't know tricks. So she stood tall, and she said,  
"I wish..." and she paused, with a thought in her head.  
"...THAT ALL OF THE DRAGONS WERE OURS!" Noir yelled fast.  
War shouted it too. "OURS! ALL OURS! OURS AT LAST!"  

Shinebright gasped. "Oh no! Once a wish has begun,  
I must finish the wish. I'm so sorry. It's done."  

## 8. WHOOSH

WHOOSH! went the wish, in a glittery whirl.  
Up went Flame! Up went Pearl! And the twins, in a twirl!  
Up went Luna and Leaf, though they tried to hold tight,  
and then Shinebright, tugged from Ruby's arms, out of sight.  

From the mountains and seas, from the clouds way up high,  
every dragon there was went whooshing on by.  

## 9. Alone

Then the meadow went quiet. The rainbow was gone.  
Ruby stood all alone on the wet, empty lawn.  
"My dragons," she whispered. A tear trickled down.  
She sat in the grass with a wobbly frown.  

But deep in her heart, she could feel, faint and far,  
every bond, like a thread, like a string to a star.  

## 10. ALL OURS

At Noir's dark palace, the dragons came down:  
on the roof, in the halls, on the stairs, all around.  
Big dragons, small dragons, striped, spotted, and bright,  
fluffy dragons, and feathery dragons in flight.  

"ALL OURS!" Noir cheered, and she twirled in her gown.  
"ALL OURS!" War cheered. "We're the bosses in town!"  

## 11. Not one would play

"Play with me, dragons!" War called. Not one dragon would play.  
They all turned their backs. They all looked away.  
"Love us!" Noir ordered. "We WISHED it! You're mine!"  
But Flame wouldn't glow, and Luna wouldn't shine.  

War's chin gave a wobble. Her eyes filled with tears.  
"They're OURS," she sniffed, "but they won't even wiggle their ears."  

*Be careful what you wish for. Wishes come true!*

## 12. AH-CHOO

Poor Black Hole was squished in the dragon-packed hall,  
with a tail on his head and his nose to the wall.  
Then a fluffy-tailed dragon went swish! past his face,  
and the fluff tickled Black Hole's nose. Uh-oh. Brace!  

"Ah... ah... AH-CHOO!" Off spun Noir's crown, far away.  
War's braid flew straight up. Every dragon went, "Hey!"  
And the sneeze shook out Shinebright's spare wish with a pop.  
It zipped out the window and over the hilltop.  

## 13. The spare wish

Then over the hills, past the creek and the trees,  
floated one little sparkle, as light as a breeze.  
It landed in Ruby's palm, soft as a kiss.  
"Shinebright's spare wish!" Ruby gasped. "Oh, look at this!"  

"I could wish them all MINE, every dragon, right here..."  
Then she stopped. "That's the same as Noir's wish. Oh dear."  

## 14. A careful wish

So Ruby thought hard. She thought all the way through.  
Then she whispered a wish that was careful and true:  
"I wish every dragon could choose where to be...  
even if they fly far, and don't come back to me."  

And she opened her hand, and she let the wish go.  
It went sparkling away, soft and golden and slow.  

*Be careful what you wish for. Wishes come true!*

## 15. They chose

POP! went the sparkle in Noir's crowded hall,  
and the wish set them free, every dragon, big and small.  
Cloud dragons flew home to the sky, way up high.  
Sea dragons went splashing, with a happy goodbye.  

And seven small dragons? They knew where to be.  
They zoomed over the hills, straight to Ruby. Whee!  
Then a heap of warm dragons came tumbling on top,  
with nuzzles and giggles that just wouldn't stop.  

## 16. Ah... ah...

And far, far away, by their quiet, dark tower,  
Noir and War argued for hour after hour.  
"It was YOUR wish!" "No, YOURS!" "You said it too fast!"  
"Well, YOU wanted them most!" "Well, YOU shouted it last!"  
Their feelings were hurt, and they both felt so blue.  
And Black Hole, between them, went... ah... ah... AH-CHOO!  

Back home, Ruby yawned, with her dragons all near,  
every one of them there because they chose to be here.  

*Be careful what you wish for. Wishes come true!*

*The End.*
