Book cover composition: the grey-blue sky jaguar cub sliding down a visible ribbon of wind out of tall moonlit clouds, grinning hugely, about to bump foreheads with the pink-haired jaguar cub who stands braced and delighted on the bottom steps of the great glowing castle stair where it meets the sea — and beneath that bottom step, a thin dark seam in the stone leaks a faint sick-green glow that neither of them has noticed. Moon flying small and joyful behind them. Perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Open sky in the upper third for the title. No rabbits, no other animals. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant.
