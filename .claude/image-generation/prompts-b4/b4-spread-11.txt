In the deepest black corner of the cave, away from the green glow: the grey-blue sky jaguar standing stone-still with his eyes closed and chin lifted in an enormous joyful sniff, grinning; one thin thread of silver moonlit air, the only silver in the whole scene, curling down from a hairline crack far up in the dark cave roof, with a single falling water drop catching the light; the pink-haired cub beside him following his gaze upward. The green cauldron-glow faint in the far background keeps the scene readable. Hope arriving as one thin silver line. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg.
