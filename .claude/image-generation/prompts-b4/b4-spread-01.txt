Wide establishing shot, vertical drama: the grand glowing spiral stair rising from the moonlit sea toward the lavender castle and an enormous perfectly round full moon (never a crescent) — but the weight of the image is at the bottom, where beneath the water line, revealed like a storybook cutaway through rock, a vast hidden cave of wet black stone sits directly under the stair's first step, utterly dark except for one tiny far-off sick-green glow. No characters. Awe, scale, and a held-breath secret; gentle, not frightening.
