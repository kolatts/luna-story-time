Warm castle kitchen interior at evening: a plump cook, a thin lamplighter and a round guard-captain all wearing the exact same small calm smile, standing oddly still and looking pleasantly at nothing; in the foreground Moon and Baby Lady exchange a worried glance with each other. Cozy golden lamplight, steam and copper pots, and something faintly wrong in the stillness. Unsettling but gentle. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
