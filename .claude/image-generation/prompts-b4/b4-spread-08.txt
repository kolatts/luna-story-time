The witch winning, seen wide in the green-lit cave: long ribbons of gold, silver, rose-pink and flame-red light streaming gently out of Moon and the small winged puppy, arcing bright through the dark and pouring down into the cauldron, where the colors sink and turn to thick glossy black like ink; the ribbons of stolen light are the brightest thing in the scene and light the witch's calm face from below, the skull-shape plain in her hair; Moon and the puppy sag, held upright by the pink-haired jaguar cub. Dramatic, flowing, sorrowful — never gory. Elysian: very old witch, one hundred and five years old, small and hunched like a hook; deeply wrinkled pale grey-lavender face with a long nose and heavy-lidded eyes; floor-length black hair that, when light shines through it, drapes into the unmistakable shape of a skull — two round sockets, a jaw, a grin — made only of falling hair, never a real skull, and it shifts when she moves; plain ragged high-collared black robe; carries a thin black wand; her cave holds a round black cauldron glowing sick green, a worn twig broom, and an enormous open spell book; no pet and no helper; ugly-scary but calm and certain, never gleeful, never gory or horrific. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg.
