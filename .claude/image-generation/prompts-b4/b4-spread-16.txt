Very quiet dawn interior, no characters anywhere: the round wooden table in pale early silver-gold light, chairs all tucked in except one pushed back a little; in front of that chair, a soup bowl clearly drunk from — half empty, spoon resting moved beside it — with the faintest last curl of steam; a few small drying rain-drops on the stone floor near the chair; the window latched, the lamps out, and through the window the perfectly round full moon (never a crescent, with the faintest thin healed line) setting pale over the sea. Crescent moon carved above the door. Stillness, tenderness, gentle mystery.
