Joy in the silver rain inside the opened cave, warm gold and indigo flooding back over the fading green: the small winged puppy, soaked and bright-eyed again, mid-argument with Moon — both of them grinning through it — Moon's golden wing held over the puppy while rain streams off it, the last grey trickle of ink washing off the puppy's brow; around them the pink-haired cub, the blue-haired sky jaguar and the three pale sisters laugh in the downpour. Wet fur, wet hair, shining puddles, pure relief and happiness. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Beedlist: pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her.
