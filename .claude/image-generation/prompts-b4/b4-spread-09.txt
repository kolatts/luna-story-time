Quiet, terrible, tender moment in the green-lit cave: the ancient witch bending gently down with one thumb extended, painting a single thin black line on the brow of the small winged puppy, who sits perfectly still and calm; Moon one step away with her hand stopped mid-reach, her face beginning to break; the puppy's round eyes gone flat and glassy-calm like a windless pond. Soft green light with bone-white accents; sad, hushed, never gory, no wounds, nothing physical wrong with the puppy. Elysian: very old witch, one hundred and five years old, small and hunched like a hook; deeply wrinkled pale grey-lavender face with a long nose and heavy-lidded eyes; floor-length black hair that, when light shines through it, drapes into the unmistakable shape of a skull — two round sockets, a jaw, a grin — made only of falling hair, never a real skull, and it shifts when she moves; plain ragged high-collared black robe; carries a thin black wand; her cave holds a round black cauldron glowing sick green, a worn twig broom, and an enormous open spell book; no pet and no helper; ugly-scary but calm and certain, never gleeful, never gory or horrific. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant.
