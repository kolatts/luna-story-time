Bright windy morning over the sea: the grey-blue sky jaguar cub sliding down a long visible ribbon of breeze from tall sunlit clouds, mid-holler with joy, while the pink-haired jaguar cub stands on a tall empty seabird rock whistling up at him, pink hair blown sideways; behind them the horizon line bright and thin where sea and sky meet. Sparkling spray, wind-blown fur, pure delight. No rabbits, no birds, no other animals. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg.
