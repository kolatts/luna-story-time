Dream-memory vision with soft dissolving edges, in faded sepia-lavender tones, framed by curling green cauldron steam: a candlelit stone hall of a college long ago, a young woman in simple ceremonial robes stepping down into a wide stone basin of dark water, gowned attendants pouring gracefully from two tall jars — and one of the jars, subtly, is the wrong one, its liquid a shade too dark, and nobody in the picture has noticed. Tender, sad, softly lit; the young woman's face hopeful, nothing frightening.
