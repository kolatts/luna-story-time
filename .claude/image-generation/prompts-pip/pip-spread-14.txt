A high wide view from a mountain road in thick white fog: Gran driving her small wooden wagon behind two patient brown horses along a broad safe road, turning the horses toward a great glow of warm gold light shining up through the fog from a distant hilltop; far below in the valley, tiny lantern lights coming out one by one at cottage doors, all of them turned the same direction. Deep indigo night, white fog, one enormous gold beacon glow in the distance. Hopeful and safe — the road is wide, the wagon steady, nobody near an edge. Only Gran — no other people, no children. Gran: an old woman with deep brown skin and silver hair pinned up, laugh lines, small round spectacles; a heavy dark-green shawl over a long grey dress; she drives a small wooden wagon pulled by two patient brown horses.
