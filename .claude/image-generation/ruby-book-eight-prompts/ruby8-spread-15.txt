Late golden afternoon outside Noir's charcoal-and-violet palace with its tall clock tower, flowering vines on the stones. In the sky above, a big clear soap-like bubble floats away carrying Ruby in a deep charcoal dress with lavender shimmer and violet swirls, waving kindly, crown and silver forehead mark visible, with Black Hole and the two-headed baby dragon (silver head and gold head) flying beside the bubble and a few small baby dragons as tiny shapes behind. In a lower palace window, War leans on the sill looking up at the bubble with a small sad, wondering face, one hand half raised as if starting to wave, her black braid super long and hanging down over the sill. Behind her in the room Noir turns away grumpily, arms crossed. Main subject: War in the window and the bubble above. Ruby: a small human girl with the proportions and appearance of a four-year-old child, warm brown skin, shoulder-length wavy rainbow hair with distinct red orange yellow green blue violet strands, shimmering golden eyes, a small flat silver birthmark shaped like an upright side-view dragon silhouette (head at top, curled tail at bottom) centered just above her eyebrows and about as tall as one of her eyes, never a jewel or tiara; a tiny simple gold crown with exactly three ball-tipped points and one small round ruby-red gem on the middle point, only about one third the width of her head, plum-purple tunic dress with gold trim, soft golden boots. She is magically newborn but walks and talks. Always fully human, two human arms and two human legs, no horns, no wings, no tail, no scales on skin. Her magical transformations change her dress and hand magic only; her rainbow hair, golden eyes and silver forehead mark remain. Noir: adult human stepmother with cool pale skin, straight chin-length black hair, tall narrow silver crown, elegant charcoal gown with violet lining and long sleeves, stern calculating expression. Rounded storybook features, never monstrous. No dragon forehead mark. War: human stepsister appearing eight years old, cool pale skin, black hair in a single high braid, dark burgundy tunic with silver belt, black boots, small angular silver tiara, stubborn scowling expression. No weapons, no dragon forehead mark. Black Hole: mischievous mean black dragon, about the size of a large dog, rounded charcoal-black scales visibly highlighted lavender, violet wing membranes, small blunt horns, four legs, curled tail and narrowed violet eyes, smug toothy grin with small blunt teeth. Clearly visible against the background. His magic is a small dark circular swirl edged with purple starlight, a storybook vacuum for objects, never cosmic horror. Nola and Marron: ONE puppy-sized baby dragon with TWO HEADS on two short necks sharing ONE rounded body, four little legs, two wings and one curled tail. Both heads are girls. The dragon is split cleanly down the middle: one head and that half of the body, one wing and that half of the tail are glittery shimmering SILVER scales (Nola, the time dragon: large deep-blue eyes, two tiny silver horns, an eager excited expression); the other head and that half of the body, the other wing and that half of the tail are glittery shimmering GOLD scales (Marron, the friendship dragon: large warm chestnut-brown eyes, two tiny gold horns, a kind patient expression). The silver and gold meet in a neat line down the chest, belly and tail; the tail tip is half silver, half gold. Smooth glittery scales like frost and sunshine, soft and rounded, no zigzag spikes, no lightning, not metal armor. Exactly two heads on one body, never two separate dragons. Dragon, never a human, never a bird.
