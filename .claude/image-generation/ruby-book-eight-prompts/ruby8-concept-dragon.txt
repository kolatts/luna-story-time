Character concept portrait of ONE baby dragon with TWO HEADS on ONE body, sitting on a lavender stone windowsill inside an old clock tower, big brass clock gears and a round glowing clock face softly behind her, warm golden light. Both heads are girls, puppy-sized, sweet and friendly, side by side on two short necks from one shared rounded body with four little legs, two wings and one curled tail.

The body is split cleanly down the middle. The LEFT head and the left half of the body, left wing and left half of the tail are glittery shimmering SILVER scales with a soft sparkle, like moonlit frost. This is Nola, the time dragon: large deep-blue eyes, two tiny silver horns, a faint silvery swirl of sparkles near her like drifting sand from an hourglass, an eager, hurry-up, excited expression, leaning forward.

The RIGHT head and the right half of the body, right wing and right half of the tail are glittery shimmering GOLD scales with a warm sparkle. This is Marron, the friendship dragon: large warm chestnut-brown eyes, two tiny gold horns, a soft warm golden glow around her, a patient, kind, cuddly expression, nuzzling her cheek against Nola's.

The silver and gold meet in a neat line down the chest, belly and tail. The tail tip is half silver, half gold. Glitter, not metal armor: soft rounded baby dragon, smooth shimmering scales, no zigzag spikes, no lightning. Dragon, never a human, never a bird. Only the one two-headed dragon in the picture, no people, no other creatures. Main subject fills most of the frame.
