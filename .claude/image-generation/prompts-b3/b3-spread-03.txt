Morning at the warm rock pool: Moon, Baby Lady and the young jaguar cub gathered close around Cheeblest, who looks out across the wide grey-blue sea toward the distant grim black stone tower silhouetted dark against the sky, its many new doors faintly visible; worried small faces, warm golden light from the castle behind them, the pool glowing gently. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower.
