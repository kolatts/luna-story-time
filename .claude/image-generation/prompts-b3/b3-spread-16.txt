Quiet night, seen from inside a warm dim candlelit room: Moon at a tall arched castle window, one hand raised to the glass in a small wave toward the sea — and far out on the moonlit horizon, a small soft grey storm-swirl standing perfectly still on the water. Hushed, tender, silver sea, one warm candle. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant.
