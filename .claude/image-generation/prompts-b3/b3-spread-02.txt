Soft grey morning on wet black sand: the dark stone tower grown strangely taller overnight, sprouting a crown of many small mismatched doors where its windows used to be; at its base Evilest hammering on the big shut main door, crown askew, mid-holler, while Beedlist stands perfectly still beside her, hands folded. Silver morning light, unsettling but gentle. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Beedlist: pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her.
