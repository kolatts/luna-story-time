Cozy warm interior at night: a round wooden table crowded with seven bowls of steaming soup under golden lamplight — Moon, Baby Lady, Cheeblest and the young jaguar cub squeezed in happily, Evilest — a little girl the same size and age as the other children, not an adult — seated with arms crossed but staying, and Beedlist, also a small girl, writing in a small black book between sips; every character is a small child — and one empty chair with a full steaming bowl waiting in front of it; crescent moon carved above the door. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Beedlist: pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her.
