Princess Moon flying fast and low over a colorless grey landscape at night, a long trail of gold sparkles and stardust streaming behind her silver mermaid tail, hair blown back — one bright warm streak of color crossing a whole grey world. In the lower corner, small and far away on the lit castle terrace, the little winged puppy with the mermaid tail has turned back and wrapped both front paws around the small round witch in a hug, the half-filled bottle glowing between them. Perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Sweeping, hopeful, cinematic. Each character appears exactly once. No other people, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Witchy: a small round elderly GOOD witch with warm brown skin, deep laugh-line crinkles and a wide kind smile, silver-white hair escaping from under a soft floppy pointed hat of patched moonlit-blue cloth; a patched blue-and-lavender travelling cloak over a simple grey dress, round spectacles, sturdy scuffed boots; she carries a fat round glass potion bottle with a wooden stopper, filled part-way with slow swirling rainbow light; warm and grandmotherly, never sinister, never green-skinned, no warts, not a scary witch.
