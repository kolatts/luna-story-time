The enormous silver-maned grey wolf walks slowly down the grey road with the whole recovered birthday feast bundled carefully in his mane — cakes and berry towers riding along safely now instead of stolen — his great head low and his ears back, embarrassed and relieved. The small curly wolf trots at his shoulder with the green scarf, tail low. Princess Moon walks beside them glowing softly gold, one hand resting on the big wolf's shoulder, delighted and trying not to smile too much. Grey woods behind, the far golden castle ahead down the road. Perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Warm and funny and kind. Wolves only — no dogs, no poodles, no other animals. Poodle: a small lean young grey WOLF (a wolf, never a dog, never a poodle) — barely a quarter the size of Hairs; a narrow clever face, tall pointed ears with one ear notched, bright amber eyes, a long bushy tail with a white tip; scruffy charcoal-and-silver fur that curls oddly tight all over him, which is how he got his name; a small tattered moss-green scarf knotted at his neck; stands and walks on four paws; a sideways scheming grin with small blunt friendly teeth; talkative, mischievous, never fierce, never frightening. Hairs: an enormous gentle grey wolf, soft dove-grey fur, glossy jet-black tail; extraordinarily long silver-grey hair growing from his head, neck and shoulders and flowing far behind him in ropes and drifts, a hundred years long and never brushed; huge soft amber eyes under kind heavy brows, rounded ears, big dark nose; mouth always closed or softly open — never bared teeth, never snarling, never fierce, never a predator; shy and slow and mountain-sized, like a hill that stood up. Gentle and sad-eyed, not frightening. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant.
