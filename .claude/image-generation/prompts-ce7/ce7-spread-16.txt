Full brilliant color at last, and the difference is the whole point: the long birthday table on the castle terrace under blazing gold lanterns — green leaves, a blue sea, orange soup, everything saturated and alive after fifteen grey pages. At one side of the table the enormous silver-maned grey wolf sits with a tiny crooked party hat perched on his huge head and his cheeks packed full, his vast mane coiled neatly under the table like a rug, and beside him the small curly wolf in the green scarf wipes his eyes with his scarf, a bowl in front of each of them. Around the table: the pink-haired winged princess and the small winged puppy with the mermaid tail standing on their chairs mid-cheer, the purple winged cat and the rose-pink winged dog, the pink-haired, blue-haired and green-haired jaguar cubs, the small round witch holding her empty bottle upside down and grinning, the old sea-foam-haired mermaid leaning in from the sea-pool, Evilest laughing, Beedlist writing in her small black book, Cheeblest beside the all-blue girl — and at the very end of the row the tall masked sister in her grey cloak, quiet, a full soup bowl set before her. Far off at the dark horizon, one small patch of the world is still washed grey. A crescent moon carved into the stone balustrade; a perfectly round full moon (never a crescent) above. Every sister is a small child except the tall masked one. Each character appears exactly once — no duplicate characters, exactly one pink-haired girl, no other children, no other people. Wolves only — no poodles. Poodle: a small lean young grey WOLF (a wolf, never a dog, never a poodle) — barely a quarter the size of Hairs; a narrow clever face, tall pointed ears with one ear notched, bright amber eyes, a long bushy tail with a white tip; scruffy charcoal-and-silver fur that curls oddly tight all over him, which is how he got his name; a small tattered moss-green scarf knotted at his neck; stands and walks on four paws; a sideways scheming grin with small blunt friendly teeth; talkative, mischievous, never fierce, never frightening. Hairs: an enormous gentle grey wolf, soft dove-grey fur, glossy jet-black tail; extraordinarily long silver-grey hair growing from his head, neck and shoulders and flowing far behind him in ropes and drifts, a hundred years long and never brushed; huge soft amber eyes under kind heavy brows, rounded ears, big dark nose; mouth always closed or softly open — never bared teeth, never snarling, never fierce, never a predator; shy and slow and mountain-sized, like a hill that stood up. Gentle and sad-eyed, not frightening. Witchy: a small round elderly GOOD witch with warm brown skin, deep laugh-line crinkles and a wide kind smile, silver-white hair escaping from under a soft floppy pointed hat of patched moonlit-blue cloth; a patched blue-and-lavender travelling cloak over a simple grey dress, round spectacles, sturdy scuffed boots; she carries a fat round glass potion bottle with a wooden stopper, filled part-way with slow swirling rainbow light; warm and grandmotherly, never sinister, never green-skinned, no warts, not a scary witch. Pearl: the oldest mermaid of the sea — a warm brown crinkled face full of kindness, long silver-white hair like sea-foam with tiny shells woven through it, kind grey-green eyes; a broad shimmering pale-green mermaid tail with mother-of-pearl scales in place of legs — no legs, tail only; no wings; a small gold crescent charm at her throat. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Dirt: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; warm soil-brown coat with dark chocolate-brown rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy leaf-green hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, calm friendly moss-green eyes, half-lidded and unhurried, a wide slow easy smile; sturdy ordinary paws lightly dusted with garden soil; no wings, no mermaid tail; a woven green-vine bracelet on one front leg. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. Beedlist: pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her. Leeblebeest: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile. Shock: a tall slender girl, a head taller than the other sisters, always standing very straight; long storm-grey hair falling past her waist; a long charcoal-grey hooded cloak whose hem fades into soft mist; grey gloves; her whole face hidden behind a smooth dark carved mask shaped like a gently scowling storm-cloud creature with two small carved fangs at its frown, round dark eye-holes, a thin hairline crack down one cheek, and a small simple tipped-sideways crescent moon — one clean thin C-shaped curve — scratched above the brow; the mask never comes off and no part of her face is ever visible; unsettling but gentle, never gory or horrific.
