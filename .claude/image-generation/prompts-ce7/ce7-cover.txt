Book cover composition: a long grey road winding out of grey woods toward Castle Everstair in the distance, the lavender castle and its great stair the only warm gold thing left in a world washed colorless (all greens, pinks and blues drained to soft grey, like a watercolor left out in the rain — but the castle's lamplight still glows warm gold and the night sky still reads deep indigo). Walking the road with her back to us, a small round grandmotherly witch in a patched blue cloak and floppy pointed hat, holding up a fat round glass bottle that swirls with the last rainbow colors left in the world. Behind her, creeping along the roadside grass, two wolves of very different sizes: a small lean curly-furred wolf in a green scarf, and behind him an ENORMOUS gentle grey wolf whose hundred-year silver hair trails away down the road behind him like a river. Far ahead over the castle, a few shining bubbles of color rise from the sea. A perfectly round full moon (never a crescent) above. Open sky in the upper third for the title. Wolves only — no dogs, no poodles, no other animals. Poodle: a small lean young grey WOLF (a wolf, never a dog, never a poodle) — barely a quarter the size of Hairs; a narrow clever face, tall pointed ears with one ear notched, bright amber eyes, a long bushy tail with a white tip; scruffy charcoal-and-silver fur that curls oddly tight all over him, which is how he got his name; a small tattered moss-green scarf knotted at his neck; stands and walks on four paws; a sideways scheming grin with small blunt friendly teeth; talkative, mischievous, never fierce, never frightening. Hairs: an enormous gentle grey wolf, soft dove-grey fur, glossy jet-black tail; extraordinarily long silver-grey hair growing from his head, neck and shoulders and flowing far behind him in ropes and drifts, a hundred years long and never brushed; huge soft amber eyes under kind heavy brows, rounded ears, big dark nose; mouth always closed or softly open — never bared teeth, never snarling, never fierce, never a predator; shy and slow and mountain-sized, like a hill that stood up. Gentle and sad-eyed, not frightening. Witchy: a small round elderly GOOD witch with warm brown skin, deep laugh-line crinkles and a wide kind smile, silver-white hair escaping from under a soft floppy pointed hat of patched moonlit-blue cloth; a patched blue-and-lavender travelling cloak over a simple grey dress, round spectacles, sturdy scuffed boots; she carries a fat round glass potion bottle with a wooden stopper, filled part-way with slow swirling rainbow light; warm and grandmotherly, never sinister, never green-skinned, no warts, not a scary witch.
