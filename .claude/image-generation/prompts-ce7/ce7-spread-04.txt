A long grey road at night winding down toward the sea, where the lavender Castle Everstair rises with its great glowing stair climbing out of the water toward a perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). The small round witch walks the road seen from behind, cloak and hat blown by the wind, her bottle of swirling rainbow light bundled in a scarf under one arm and glowing through the cloth. The whole landscape is washed colorless grey — except the castle's warm gold windows and lanterns far ahead, and the little rainbow glow at her elbow. Down in the dark water, faint shapes of mermaid tails are already turning toward the castle. No other people, no other animals. Witchy: a small round elderly GOOD witch with warm brown skin, deep laugh-line crinkles and a wide kind smile, silver-white hair escaping from under a soft floppy pointed hat of patched moonlit-blue cloth; a patched blue-and-lavender travelling cloak over a simple grey dress, round spectacles, sturdy scuffed boots; she carries a fat round glass potion bottle with a wooden stopper, filled part-way with slow swirling rainbow light; warm and grandmotherly, never sinister, never green-skinned, no warts, not a scary witch.
