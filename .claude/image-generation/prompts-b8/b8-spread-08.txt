Seen from far away across the night sea: one single enormous translucent blue bubble, bigger than the whole castle, has rolled over lavender Castle Everstair and its great stair. Inside the bubble the castle, the stair, the sea and the perfectly round full moon are all tinted shades of blue, every color turned to blue, while outside the bubble the night is still deep indigo and lavender. Along the sea road in the foreground a small all-blue girl walks calmly through the bubble's wall toward the castle. Far behind her, tiny, the hunched old witch stands beside her cauldron glowing sick green. No other people, no animals. Blooey: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile. Elysian: very old witch, one hundred and five years old, small and hunched like a hook; deeply wrinkled pale grey-lavender face with a long nose and heavy-lidded eyes; floor-length black hair that, when light shines through it, drapes into the unmistakable shape of a skull — two round sockets, a jaw, a grin — made only of falling hair strands, never a real skull, and it shifts when she moves; the skull shape appears ONLY in her hair, never on her clothing; plain ragged high-collared black robe with no pattern, no markings, and no skull on it; carries a thin black wand; her cave holds a round black cauldron glowing sick green, a worn twig broom, and an enormous open spell book; no pet and no helper; ugly-scary but calm and certain, never gleeful, never gory or horrific.
