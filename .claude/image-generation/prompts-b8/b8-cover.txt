Book cover composition: the great glowing stair of lavender Castle Everstair climbing out of the night sea toward a perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). On the stair, Princess Moon hovers holding up a small round lavender purse with a brightly shining gold clasp, and beside her Baby Lady the winged mermaid-puppy cups a tiny glowing round puff of light in her front paws. All around them, dozens of large slow translucent blue bubbles drift up the stair and into the sky, and wherever a bubble has touched, one step or one patch of stone has turned bright blue. Far down at the bottom step where the stair meets the sea, small and calm, stands an all-blue girl in a midnight-blue dress, watching. Open sky in the upper third for the title. No other people, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Blooey: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile.
