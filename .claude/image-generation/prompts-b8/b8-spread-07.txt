The bottom step of the great stair at night, where the lavender stone meets the dark sea, lit by one warm gold lantern. The small all-blue girl stands on the bottom step, blue in the lamplight, hands folded, calm, asking. Princess Moon hovers one step above, looking gently down at her, and above the blue girl's head a soft dream-cloud shows what Moon sees: a very long table with many chairs and small blue figures seated all along it. Baby Lady peeks out from behind Moon. A perfectly round full moon over the sea (never a crescent). A quiet, sad, kind moment; nothing frightening. No other people, no other animals. Blooey: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
