A long soup table on the castle terrace at night in full warm color, gold lanterns, steaming bowls, the great stair rising behind toward a perfectly round full yellow moon (never a crescent). Seated along the table, each exactly once: Princess Moon and Baby Lady side by side; Purple Shine and Pink Shine; the three young jaguar cubs (pink tuft, blue tuft, green tuft); the small curly grey wolf and, taking up the whole far side, the enormous long-haired gentle wolf; and the sisters in a row — Evilest, Cheeblest, Beedlist — with the small all-blue girl seated in the middle of them rather than at the end. Far off at the edge of the sea, tiny at the horizon, stands the tall masked grey-cloaked sister. At the near end of the table one full, untouched soup bowl sits before an empty chair. No other people, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Dirt: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; warm soil-brown coat with dark chocolate-brown rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy leaf-green hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, calm friendly moss-green eyes, half-lidded and unhurried, a wide slow easy smile; sturdy ordinary paws lightly dusted with garden soil; no wings, no mermaid tail; a woven green-vine bracelet on one front leg. Poodle: a small lean young grey WOLF (a wolf, never a dog, never a poodle) — barely a quarter the size of Hairs; a narrow clever face, tall pointed ears with one ear notched, bright amber eyes, a long bushy tail with a white tip; scruffy charcoal-and-silver fur that curls oddly tight all over him, which is how he got his name; a small tattered moss-green scarf knotted at his neck; stands and walks on four paws; a sideways scheming grin with small blunt friendly teeth; talkative, mischievous, never fierce, never frightening. Hairs: an enormous gentle grey wolf, soft dove-grey fur, glossy jet-black tail; extraordinarily long silver-grey hair growing from his head, neck and shoulders and flowing far behind him in ropes and drifts, a hundred years long and never brushed; huge soft amber eyes under kind heavy brows, rounded ears, big dark nose; mouth always closed or softly open — never bared teeth, never snarling, never fierce, never a predator; shy and slow and mountain-sized, like a hill that stood up. Gentle and sad-eyed, not frightening. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. Beedlist: pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her. Blooey: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile. Shock: a tall slender girl, a head taller than the other sisters, always standing very straight; long storm-grey hair falling past her waist; a long charcoal-grey hooded cloak whose hem fades into soft mist; grey gloves; her whole face hidden behind a smooth dark carved mask shaped like a gently scowling storm-cloud creature with two small carved fangs at its frown, round dark eye-holes, a thin hairline crack down one cheek, and a small simple tipped-sideways crescent moon — one clean thin C-shaped curve — scratched above the brow; the mask never comes off and no part of her face is ever visible; unsettling but gentle, never gory or horrific.
