The castle terrace beside the warm round pool at bright morning, lavender stone and gold light, the sea beyond. Princess Moon has just pulled a tiny glowing golden ladder out of the small lavender purse; the ladder leads up into a little cloud that is only half painted in. Above floats a kite of an impossible shifting rainbow color, and a small curl of gold musical sparkle drifts out of the purse like a folded-up song. Baby Lady holds a small fuzzy round glowing puff of soft light in her front paws (a glowing puff, not an animal, no face). A rock on the terrace has just turned bright pink. Pink Shine watches from the pool's edge, smiling. The round full moon faint and pale in the day sky. No other people, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark.
