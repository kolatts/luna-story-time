Inside the giant bubble fort, the whole world soft blue and out of focus in the background. In sharp focus in the center: Baby Lady, who has just tapped herself with the small purse held in her mouth, is now truly, fully blue — her fur, ears, wings and mermaid tail all the same smooth soft matte blue as the blue girl — sitting up straight and proud. The small all-blue girl has gone down on her knees to be level with her, one hand just reaching out toward the puppy, her face open and amazed. The purse's tiny gold clasp glints. A perfectly round blue-tinted full moon above (never a crescent). Nobody else. No other people, no other animals. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Blooey: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile.
