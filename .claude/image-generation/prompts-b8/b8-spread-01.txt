The twins' bedroom in lavender Castle Everstair at pink morning: lavender stone walls, warm gold light through a round arched window, and outside the window the great stair climbing up toward a perfectly round full moon still faint in the dawn sky. On a plump pillow sits a small round lavender-and-gold purse with a strap and a brightly shining gold clasp. Princess Moon and Baby Lady lean over the pillow, wide-eyed and curious. Purple Shine hovers in the doorway behind them, gentle and watchful. In the far upper corner, glimpsed through the window at the very edge of the horizon where the dark begins, one tiny desaturated pocket of grey grass and grey stones, colorless in a colored world. No other people, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you.
