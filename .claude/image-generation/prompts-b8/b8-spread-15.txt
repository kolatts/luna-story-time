The castle garden at night in full warm color, with small gold flowers glowing among the bean rows. A cozy little pillow fort made of two blankets draped over one chair, lit from inside by a gold lantern, with a small blank wooden sign propped at its doorway (the sign is completely blank: no letters, no writing, no marks). The small all-blue girl sits inside the fort, content, and Baby Lady, back to her usual golden-cream colors except for one ear that is still faintly blue, is crawling in to join her. The lavender castle and the great stair behind, under a perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Nobody else. No other people, no other animals. Blooey: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
