A cozy castle bathroom at night: lavender stone, a round stone tub full of warm lamplit water and ordinary white foam, a gold lantern. Big slow translucent blue bubbles drift in through an arched window from the stair outside. One blue bubble is popping on the tip of Princess Moon's golden wing while a small gold spark is being drawn inside another bubble that is closing up around it; another bubble pops on Baby Lady's nose. Patches of the room have turned bright blue where bubbles touched: one blue step, a blue towel on a hook, a blue patch of ceiling. Moon's face is turning from delight to alarm; Baby Lady's ears are flat. Through the window, a perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). No other people, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
