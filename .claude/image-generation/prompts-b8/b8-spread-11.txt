A quiet close two-shot in the middle of the castle courtyard inside the giant bubble fort, everything tinted shades of blue and softly lit: Baby Lady, small, sitting up and looking upward, drawn exactly as herself but with all her colors tinted blue for this scene, and the small all-blue girl looking down at her. The small purse lies on the blue stone beside Baby Lady, its gold clasp the only faint gold glint. A perfectly round blue-tinted full moon behind them (never a crescent). Nobody else in the picture. Gentle, still, close. No other people, no other animals. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Blooey: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile.
