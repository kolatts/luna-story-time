The castle garden inside the giant bubble fort at night, every single thing tinted shades of blue, including every character: all three young jaguar cubs, Princess Moon and Baby Lady are drawn exactly as themselves but with all their colors tinted blue for this scene. The young jaguar cub with the pink tuft rears up clawing at the stretchy translucent bubble wall, which dents inward like jelly around her paws; the cub with the blue tuft beside her blows a gust that ripples the wall; the cub with the green tuft lies flat in the bean rows with new sprouts coming up blue around him. Princess Moon kneels with the small purse, holding up a blue ladder, a blue kite and a blue glowing puff of light, all useless. Baby Lady sits a little apart, thinking. A perfectly round blue-tinted full moon above (never a crescent). No rabbits, no other animals, no other people. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Dirt: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; warm soil-brown coat with dark chocolate-brown rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy leaf-green hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, calm friendly moss-green eyes, half-lidded and unhurried, a wide slow easy smile; sturdy ordinary paws lightly dusted with garden soil; no wings, no mermaid tail; a woven green-vine bracelet on one front leg. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
