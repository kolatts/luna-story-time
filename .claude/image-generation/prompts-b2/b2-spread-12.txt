One single young jaguar cub climbing a sheer wet black rock face out of a crashing night sea, small curved claws dug firmly into the stone, fur soaked, pink hair tuft plastered down, expression determined; above her at the summit sits one wide empty black stone basin glinting faintly with swirling gathered light. Sea spray, effort, courage. The jaguar cub: soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. She is the ONLY animal in the image. No rabbits, no bunnies, no other animals, no people.
