The rock pool in golden evening light: Moon and Baby Lady floating happily, tails in the water, faces tipped up; Cheeblest sitting on the pool's stone rim with her feet in, laughing — but her eyes drifting toward a tiny dark tower far across the sea. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower.
