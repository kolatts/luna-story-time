Cheeblest alone on a narrow stone causeway between two shores, wind pulling her bows sideways, small fists clenched, shouting bravely across the water; the dark tower far on one side, the warm pool-glow far on the other. The middle of everything. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower.
