Deep underwater, blue-black and vast: Cottontail sinking small through the dark with tiny bubbles, one thin shaft of moonlight reaching her — her eyes open, calm, looking at her own outstretched paw where small claws catch the light. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg.
