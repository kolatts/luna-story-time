Night shore: Evilest raging comically, crown askew, fists balled — while Beedlist stands perfectly still all in black beside her with a very small calm smile, writing in a small black book; the sea behind them unnaturally flat. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Beedlist: pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her.
