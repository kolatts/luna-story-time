A far shore of wet black sand under an indigo night sky: a crowned girl mid-shout with her mouth wide open, fists raised, stamping, the water around her churning with angry ripples and rings — and beside her a smaller girl standing utterly still, hands folded, the sea directly in front of her flat as glass; a grim black tower behind them. Contrast of storm and stillness, quietly ominous but not scary for children. The shouting girl: pale, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown. The still girl: dressed entirely in black, long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, no crown, no color anywhere on her, small calm unreadable face, hands folded in front of her.
