Triumphant flood of light: the black basin split by one thin bright scratch, reflections pouring out as ribbons of light streaming home in every direction across the night sea; Cottontail silhouetted small and proud against the flood. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg.
