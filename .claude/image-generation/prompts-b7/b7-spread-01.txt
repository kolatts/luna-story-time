Wide establishing view at golden dusk: a small cozy village of round thatched cottages with warm lit windows at the bottom of a green hill; at the top of the hill a tall arched gate woven entirely of dark brambles and thorns, and beyond it a deep misty blue-green wood going on forever. Nobody visible, empty lane, warm and storybook, a little mysterious but safe. Perfectly round full moon low in the sky (never a crescent). No people, no animals.
