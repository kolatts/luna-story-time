A memory picture, softly faded at the edges like an old page: inside a big cozy hollow-tree den, a small fire and a covered pot; three empty wooden bowls set neatly on a low stone table, waiting, a little dusty. Through the round doorway, far off, the enormous grey wolf is seen from behind walking away from the distant thorn gate with his head down, endless silver hair trailing behind him. Warm gold firelight against deep blue-green evening. Lonely but tender. No people, no other animals. Hairs: an enormous gentle grey wolf, soft dove-grey fur, glossy jet-black tail; extraordinarily long silver-grey hair growing from his head, neck and shoulders and flowing far behind him in ropes and drifts, a hundred years long and never brushed; huge soft amber eyes under kind heavy brows, rounded ears, big dark nose; mouth always closed or softly open — never bared teeth, never snarling, never fierce, never a predator; shy and slow and mountain-sized, like a hill that stood up. Gentle and sad-eyed, not frightening.
