Sunny afternoon in a tangle of blackberry brambles at the edge of a meadow: Princess Fern reaching up on tiptoe to drop a fat blackberry into her woven basket, her tin crown sliding sideways; Baby Bee sitting in the grass beside her, blanket in her lap, cheeks and hands purple with berry juice, looking straight at the viewer. Warm gold light, sparkles in the air, deeply cozy. No other children and no other people in the picture; each character appears exactly once. No other animals. Princess Fern: a seven-year-old girl, warm brown skin, dark curly hair in two round puffs, a small crooked tin crown sitting slightly askew; moss-green hooded cloak over a mustard-yellow dress, sturdy brown boots, a small woven berry basket; big dark eyes, a determined chin. Baby Bee: a two-year-old toddler girl, warm brown skin, one wispy curl on top of her head, very round cheeks, a honey-yellow knitted romper with tiny round buttons, bare feet; always holding a soft grey knitted blanket; calm, unbothered, enormous dark eyes.
