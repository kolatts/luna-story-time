Big warm final scene: a long table set right in the clearing of the Bramblewood under strings of little glowing lanterns, loaded with bowls and bread and blackberries; villagers in cloaks and shawls seated all along it, laughing and passing food; the enormous grey wolf curled around the far end like a grey hillside, chin on the table, eyes closed in bliss, his single shining silver braid winding away down a path through the trees; Princess Fern standing on the bench with her crooked crown, and Baby Bee holding the very end of the braid. Golden lantern light, deep indigo night above, sparkles and stardust, perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Open sky in the upper third. Joyful and cozy. Hairs: an enormous gentle grey wolf, soft dove-grey fur, glossy jet-black tail; extraordinarily long silver-grey hair growing from his head, neck and shoulders and flowing far behind him in ropes and drifts, a hundred years long and never brushed; huge soft amber eyes under kind heavy brows, rounded ears, big dark nose; mouth always closed or softly open — never bared teeth, never snarling, never fierce, never a predator; shy and slow and mountain-sized, like a hill that stood up. Gentle and sad-eyed, not frightening. Princess Fern: a seven-year-old girl, warm brown skin, dark curly hair in two round puffs, a small crooked tin crown sitting slightly askew; moss-green hooded cloak over a mustard-yellow dress, sturdy brown boots, a small woven berry basket; big dark eyes, a determined chin. Baby Bee: a two-year-old toddler girl, warm brown skin, one wispy curl on top of her head, very round cheeks, a honey-yellow knitted romper with tiny round buttons, bare feet; always holding a soft grey knitted blanket; calm, unbothered, enormous dark eyes.
