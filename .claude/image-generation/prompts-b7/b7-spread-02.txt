A storybook rumor-picture: the enormous grey wolf shown far away and small among misty blue-green trees, mostly a silhouette with two soft amber eyes glowing warmly and his impossibly long silver hair trailing off between the trunks into the fog. Dreamy, hushed, curious rather than scary — the wolf is calm and still, ears soft, mouth closed. No people, no other animals. Hairs: an enormous gentle grey wolf, soft dove-grey fur, glossy jet-black tail; extraordinarily long silver-grey hair growing from his head, neck and shoulders and flowing far behind him in ropes and drifts, a hundred years long and never brushed; huge soft amber eyes under kind heavy brows, rounded ears, big dark nose; mouth always closed or softly open — never bared teeth, never snarling, never fierce, never a predator; shy and slow and mountain-sized, like a hill that stood up. Gentle and sad-eyed, not frightening.
