Night at the castle's great arched wooden front door, warm gold light spilling out onto the steps: on the doorstep stands the all-blue girl, calm and unhurried, holding up the small clay pot now brimming with bright swirling gold light that lights her blue face from below; in the warm glowing doorway stand Moon and the small winged puppy, tired and dim, looking up at her; and up the castle steps behind the blue girl races Cheeblest, taking them two at a time, hair bows flying loose, mouth open mid-warning, one arm pointing at the blue girl. Every sister is a small child. No other children and no other people anywhere in the image — exactly these four characters, each appearing exactly once. Deep indigo night, perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Dramatic but gentle, never gory. Leeblebeest: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile; she carries a little round clay pot of plain unglazed brown clay, small as a teacup, cupped carefully in both hands. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
