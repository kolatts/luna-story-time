The first pale gold of dawn over the castle garden: the family still asleep in their soil rows while ribbons of gold light curl up out of the earth around them like glowing pea-vine tendrils, winding gently back into Moon's wings — which are beginning to glow bright again — and around the small winged puppy's long curly ears; new green sprouts standing up all along the rows; at the row's end the soil-brown jaguar cub with leaf-green hair sleeps too, one paw resting over a seedling. Green and gold sprouting through the fading indigo night, hushed and full of quiet magic. Each character appears exactly once — no duplicate characters. No rabbits, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Dirt: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; warm soil-brown coat with dark chocolate-brown rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy leaf-green hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, calm friendly moss-green eyes, half-lidded and unhurried, a wide slow easy smile; sturdy ordinary paws lightly dusted with garden soil; no wings, no mermaid tail; a woven green-vine bracelet on one front leg.
