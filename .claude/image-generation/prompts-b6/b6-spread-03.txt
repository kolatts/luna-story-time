Garden tour in mellow late-morning light: the soil-brown jaguar cub with leaf-green hair proudly holding up one very small brown seed between two claws to show his visitors; Moon crouched down gently poking at a little earthworm, the small winged puppy watching with polite attention, the blue-haired sky jaguar mid-yawn, the pink-haired jaguar cub squinting at a bean pole with polite doubt; rows of green sprouts and rich brown soil beds around them, the lavender castle rising behind. Gently funny, warm earth browns and garden greens. No rabbits, no other animals. Dirt: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; warm soil-brown coat with dark chocolate-brown rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy leaf-green hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, calm friendly moss-green eyes, half-lidded and unhurried, a wide slow easy smile; sturdy ordinary paws lightly dusted with garden soil; no wings, no mermaid tail; a woven green-vine bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg.
