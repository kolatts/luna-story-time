Soft early morning in the garden: the all-blue girl sitting slumped and tired among the blossoming broken pieces of her clay pot — the clay bits sprouting little green shoots and small gold flowers around her — while Cheeblest kneels close beside her, patting the warm brown earth in invitation, her hair bows crooked from running, her smile kind; the first morning gold touching both girls' faces. Every sister is a small child. Gentle, forgiving, warm earth browns and soft golds. Never gory. Leeblebeest: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile; she carries a little round clay pot of plain unglazed brown clay, small as a teacup, cupped carefully in both hands. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower.
