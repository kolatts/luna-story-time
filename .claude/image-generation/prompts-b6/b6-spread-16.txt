A golden late-afternoon garden party at Castle Everstair: one long crowded table set right between the garden rows — steaming soup bowls, platters of green beans, a tall slightly-crooked cake; Moon and the small winged puppy standing on their chairs mid-gift, sprinkling gold sparkles down onto the soil-brown jaguar cub with leaf-green hair, who grins his wide slow grin as tiny green sprouts pop up around his paws; around the table the small all-purple cat, the fluffy rose-pink dog, the pink-haired jaguar cub and the blue-haired sky jaguar mid-cheer, Evilest laughing, Beedlist writing in her small black book, Cheeblest sitting beside the all-blue girl who blinks happily in the daylight — and at the far end of the row the tall masked sister in her grey cloak, present and unremarked, a soup bowl set before her. Every sister is a small child except the tall masked one. A crescent moon carved on a wooden garden arch; a perfectly round full moon (never a crescent) rising pale beyond the castle. Warm, crowded, golden, safe. Each character appears exactly once — no duplicate characters, exactly one pink-haired girl. No rabbits, no other animals. Dirt: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; warm soil-brown coat with dark chocolate-brown rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy leaf-green hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, calm friendly moss-green eyes, half-lidded and unhurried, a wide slow easy smile; sturdy ordinary paws lightly dusted with garden soil; no wings, no mermaid tail; a woven green-vine bracelet on one front leg. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. Beedlist: pale girl dressed entirely in black: long straight black hair, plain long black high-collared dress, black shoes, black ribbon; no crown; no color anywhere on her; small calm unreadable face; perfectly still posture, hands folded in front of her. Shock: a tall slender girl, a head taller than the other sisters, always standing very straight; long storm-grey hair falling past her waist; a long charcoal-grey hooded cloak whose hem fades into soft mist; grey gloves; her whole face hidden behind a smooth dark carved mask shaped like a gently scowling storm-cloud creature with two small carved fangs at its frown, round dark eye-holes, a thin hairline crack down one cheek, and a small simple tipped-sideways crescent moon — one clean thin C-shaped curve — scratched above the brow; the mask never comes off and no part of her face is ever visible; unsettling but gentle, never gory or horrific. Leeblebeest: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile; she carries a little round clay pot of plain unglazed brown clay, small as a teacup, cupped carefully in both hands.
