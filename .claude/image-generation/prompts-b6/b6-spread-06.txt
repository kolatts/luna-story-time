Tall vertical storybook composition split by the castle wall: in the upper half, the twins' cozy curtained bedroom at nap time, lit by soft afternoon light through the curtains — Moon flopped grumpily across her bed mid-huff with blankets kicked everywhere, the small winged puppy holding a pillow over her long ears, a little moth tapping at the windowpane; from Moon one tiny bright gold spark escapes and rolls in a thin dotted sparkle-trail down through the castle stones; in the lower, darker half the trail ends at a small clay pot held up by two blue hands, the single gold drop falling in, the all-blue girl's face lit faintly gold from below with a soft smile. Warm lamplit top, deep indigo bottom lit only by the pot's gold glow. Never frightening. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Leeblebeest: a girl the same size as the other small sisters; every bit of her skin is a smooth soft matte blue — face, ears, hands, all of it, like a girl painted gently blue — strange and calm but never frightening, never gory; long straight deep-blue hair; plain long midnight-blue high-collared dress; a quiet patient face with a small unreadable smile; she carries a little round clay pot of plain unglazed brown clay, small as a teacup, cupped carefully in both hands.
