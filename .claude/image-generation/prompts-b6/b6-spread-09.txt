A grey, tired morning at the castle: Moon sitting small on the terrace steps with her golden wings drooping and dim, lifting halfway and settling again; the small winged puppy beside her saying something tiny, while below them little fish lean up out of the calm sea to listen; behind the twins the small all-purple cat wraps a warm blanket around both of them and the fluffy rose-pink dog nudges over a steaming bowl of soup; all the usual sparkle faint and thin. Muted gold and soft grey over lavender stone. Tender, quiet, safe. No rabbits, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark.
