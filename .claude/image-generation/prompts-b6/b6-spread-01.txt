Warm gold morning at Castle Everstair: the lavender castle bright with sunrise over a calm sea, the whole family together at breakfast on the wide castle terrace — Moon mid-enormous-yawn with one hand over her mouth, the small winged puppy yawning back beside her, the small all-purple cat and the fluffy rose-pink dog pouring tea, the pink-haired jaguar cub and the blue-haired sky jaguar tumbling in play by the balustrade; below the terrace, garden beds of warm brown soil and green rows just visible. Cozy, golden, safe. No rabbits, no other animals. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg.
