Cozy interior at night: a round wooden table under warm lamplight set with four bowls of steaming soup. On one side a young pink-haired winged mermaid princess and a winged mermaid puppy; across from them a girl in a spiky black crown and a girl in purple with bow pigtails, both sitting awkward and small but staying. A crescent moon carved above the door. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. Warm, gentle, a family beginning.
