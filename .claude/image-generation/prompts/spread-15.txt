Warm triumphant reveal: the top of a great stair at a castle gate, every window and lamp blazing with golden light against the night, and one hundred tiny silver moth-knights arrayed in neat glowing ranks, wings up. At the front stands their captain, wings trembling with joy. Captain Pim: palm-sized silver moth-knight with moth wings and a tiny lantern-shaped helm. Overwhelming warmth after cold, welcome home.
