Book cover composition: a young winged mermaid princess and a tiny winged mermaid puppy together on a castle balcony, a sparkle trail spiraling up from the girl's hand, lavender castle spires and an enormous glowing full moon behind them — and tiny in the far bottom corner, a dark tower where two small figures watch. Central, iconic, inviting. Leave the upper area open sky for a title to be added later. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
