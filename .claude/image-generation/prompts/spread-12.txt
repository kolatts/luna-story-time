A thin ribbon of pink-gold dream light slipping through a hairline crack in a black glass lantern, streaming out into the indigo night air — and nearby a pale girl with black pigtail bows in a deep-purple dress lifting her face in wonder as the ribbon of light reaches her cheek. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower.
