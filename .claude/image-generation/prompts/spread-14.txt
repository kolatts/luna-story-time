Long vertical view of an endless spiral stair seen from far away, climbing through an enormous night sky of stars and clouds toward the moon. Two very small figures walk up it, a tiny puppy leading a girl, no glow from their wings, tired but determined. Vast sky, small brave travelers. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Their wings are dim and not glowing.
