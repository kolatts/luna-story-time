Split composition: a shining lavender castle stair climbing on the left, and a squat grim black-stone tower on the right where moonlight refuses to reach. Two small figures stand on the tower battlement against the sky. Evilest: pale girl, black hair, tall spiky black crown, high-collared black gown, arms crossed, sharp confident smile. Cheeblest: pale girl, black hair in two bows and pigtails, deep-purple dress, watchful rather than cruel, eyes slightly too soft for the tower. The girls are small in frame, distant.
