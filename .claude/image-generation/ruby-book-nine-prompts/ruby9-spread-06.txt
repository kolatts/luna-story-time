Bright sunny daytime on a green meadow lawn just outside the lavender-stone castle of Riverlight, blue sky, buttercups and daisies, a low lavender-stone wall with an open gate in the background. Ruby in her normal PLUM tunic dress stands calmly with one hand tapping her own heart, speaking gently, crown and silver forehead mark visible. On the grass in front of her sits the Spirit Egg, one big pumpkin-sized egg with a rainbow-banded shell, now dim and quiet: its colors are pale and faded, with no glow and no sparkles, shut tight, not cracked. Flame breathes a tiny warm puff on it, Pearl hums beside it, and the two-headed baby dragon (silver head and gold head) twirls a little silver sparkle at it, all three worried. Shinebright sits close, looking at the egg thoughtfully. Exactly Ruby, Flame, Pearl, Shinebright and the two-headed dragon. No Noir, no War. Ruby: a small human girl with the proportions and appearance of a four-year-old child, warm brown skin, shoulder-length wavy rainbow hair with distinct red orange yellow green blue violet strands, shimmering golden eyes, a small flat silver birthmark shaped like an upright side-view dragon silhouette (head at top, curled tail at bottom) centered just above her eyebrows and about as tall as one of her eyes, never a jewel or tiara; a tiny simple gold crown with exactly three ball-tipped points and one small round ruby-red gem on the middle point, only about one third the width of her head, plum-purple tunic dress with gold trim, soft golden boots. She is magically newborn but walks and talks. Always fully human, two human arms and two human legs, no horns, no wings, no tail, no scales on skin. Her magical transformations change her dress and hand magic only; her rainbow hair, golden eyes and silver forehead mark remain. Flame: female baby fire dragon the size of a small puppy, rounded red body, orange cheek frills and tiny orange wings, buttery yellow belly and short yellow horns, large amber eyes, four little legs and one curled red tail, playful snuggly expression. Dragon, never a human. Tiny friendly flame puffs only when specified. Pearl: female baby water dragon the size of a small puppy, pearl-white rounded body, sky-blue ears and wing membranes, blue back ridge and curled blue-tipped tail, large blue eyes, four tiny legs, clear transparent soap-like bubbles floating around her. Dragon, never a human. No fire, no colored opaque bubbles. Shinebright: female baby wish dragon the size of a small puppy, rounded body whose back, head, legs and the tops of her paws are swirled sea-GREEN and ocean-BLUE scales; glittering sparkly SILVER on her belly, the underside of her neck, the underside of her tail, her wing membranes and the pads under her paws; a row of small glittery spikes down her back from head to tail, alternating SILVER and GOLD; two tiny silver horns, large bright sea-green eyes with gold flecks, four little legs and one curled green-blue tail with a silver underside, sweet bright smile. A soft glitter shimmer rims her outline. Dragon, never a human, never a fish, never a bird. Gold-and-silver wish glitter only when specified. Nola and Marron: ONE puppy-sized baby dragon with TWO HEADS on two short necks sharing ONE rounded body, four little legs, two wings and one curled tail. Both heads are girls. The dragon is split cleanly down the middle: one head and that half of the body, one wing and that half of the tail are glittery shimmering SILVER scales (Nola, the time dragon: large deep-blue eyes, two tiny silver horns, an eager excited expression); the other head and that half of the body, the other wing and that half of the tail are glittery shimmering GOLD scales (Marron, the friendship dragon: large warm chestnut-brown eyes, two tiny gold horns, a kind patient expression). The silver and gold meet in a neat line down the chest, belly and tail; the tail tip is half silver, half gold. Smooth glittery scales like frost and sunshine, soft and rounded, no zigzag spikes, no lightning, not metal armor. Exactly two heads on one body, never two separate dragons. Dragon, never a human, never a bird.
