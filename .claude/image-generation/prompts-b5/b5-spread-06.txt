The black unicorn standing close at the near end of the bare sea road, in full moonlight: black all over, coat and long mane and spiral horn, edged in cold silver rim-light so she reads clearly against the indigo night, large pale silver eyes; her head lowered to speak, one thin white glassy ring of sound just beginning around her; before her on the glowing bottom steps, Moon and the small winged puppy stand small but unbowed. Perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Beautiful and frightening like a storm, never gory. The unicorn: a slender unicorn, black all over — black coat, long black mane and tail, long black spiral horn — always edged in cold silver moonlight rim-light so she reads clearly against the night; large pale silver eyes; beautiful and wrong at once, like a hole shaped like a unicorn; when she screams, thin white glassy rings of sound ripple visibly outward from her; frightening only the way a storm is frightening, never gory, never bloody. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
