The twins singing side by side on the very bottom step at night, eyes closed, heads tilted up, soft gold music-sparkles rising from them like fireflies; from their chests two thin threads of light run out low across the dark calm sea toward opposite horizons — one thread glowing violet-purple, one glowing rose-pink — vanishing into the far distance; the sea holding its breath. Perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Hushed, hopeful, luminous. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
