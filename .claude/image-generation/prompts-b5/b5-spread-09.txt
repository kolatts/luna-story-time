On the glowing bottom steps at night: Moon sitting down small with her wings drooping, while the small winged puppy stands square in front of her, chest out, one front paw raised mid-speech, tiny and utterly determined; beside them the pink-haired jaguar cub gestures at her own back with both front paws mid-realization, and the blue-haired sky jaguar nods hard. Warm gold lamplight from the castle above against the indigo night. Perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Grief turning into courage; never gory. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. Baby Jag Cottontail: young jaguar cub, soft yellow-gold coat with black rosette spots, a tuft of bright pink wavy hair, warm amber eyes, sturdy ordinary paws with small curved claws; no wings and no mermaid tail; a woven seashell bracelet on one front leg. Winds: young jaguar cub the same size and age as Cottontail; pale grey-blue coat with cloud-white rosette spots, a tuft of bright wavy blue hair exactly the shape of Cottontail's pink tuft, very wide-open sky-blue eyes, a huge cheerful grin; sturdy ordinary paws; no wings, no mermaid tail, no magic, no sparkle; a woven pale-blue grass bracelet on one front leg.
