At the foot of the great glowing stair where it meets the sea, soft gold morning light: two newcomers climbing dripping out of the water onto the bottom steps — a small all-purple cat mid-bow and a fluffy rose-pink dog beside her, both with folded fairy wings and mermaid tails trailing seawater, both smiling warm and certain; above them on the steps Moon and the small winged puppy lean down, curious and delighted; two rings of ripples widening on the sea behind. Sparkling spray. No rabbits, no other animals. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
