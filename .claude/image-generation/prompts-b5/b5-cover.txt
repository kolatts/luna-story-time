Book cover composition: climbing the great glowing castle stair out of the moonlit sea, two dripping newcomers — a small all-purple cat mid-step and a fluffy rose-pink dog beside her, fairy wings folded, mermaid tails trailing seawater — looking up with warm knowing smiles at Moon, who hovers above the steps in delight with the small winged puppy beside her; far out at the dark horizon behind them, tiny, a black unicorn silhouette edged in silver watches, one thin white ring of sound around her. Perfectly round full moon (never a crescent). Open sky in the upper third for the title. No rabbits, no other animals. Purple Shine: small sleek cat, purple all over — plum-purple fur, deeper violet ears; large gentle violet eyes; delicate translucent lavender fairy wings, small and fine; her cat body ends in a shimmering amethyst-purple mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver necklace with a tiny purple star charm; calm, warm, watchful, like someone who already knows you. Pink Shine: fluffy grown dog, warm rose-pink all over, long soft curly ears; kind dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-pink fairy wings; his dog body ends in a rose-gold mermaid tail in place of back legs — no back legs, no back paws, front paws only; a thin silver collar with a tiny pink star charm; steady and gentle, with a woolly deep bark. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar. The unicorn: a slender unicorn, black all over — black coat, long black mane and tail, long black spiral horn — always edged in cold silver moonlight rim-light so she reads clearly against the night; large pale silver eyes; beautiful and wrong at once, like a hole shaped like a unicorn; when she screams, thin white glassy rings of sound ripple visibly outward from her; frightening only the way a storm is frightening, never gory, never bloody.
