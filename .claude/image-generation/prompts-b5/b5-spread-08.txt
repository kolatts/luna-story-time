Close, quiet menace on the moonlit shore: the black unicorn leaning her long neck down until her pale silver eyes are level with Moon and the small winged puppy, one soft white ring of sound drifting off her like smoke; the twins' faces caught at the exact moment of understanding — eyes going wide, the gasp, moonlight on tears just starting. Deep indigo and silver, very still; the fear lives in the stillness, never gory. The unicorn: a slender unicorn, black all over — black coat, long black mane and tail, long black spiral horn — always edged in cold silver moonlight rim-light so she reads clearly against the night; large pale silver eyes; beautiful and wrong at once, like a hole shaped like a unicorn; when she screams, thin white glassy rings of sound ripple visibly outward from her; frightening only the way a storm is frightening, never gory, never bloody. Princess Moon: young girl, long wavy pink hair, large dark-blue eyes, rosy cheeks; delicate translucent golden fairy wings with fine filigree veining; silver iridescent mermaid tail with pearl-sheen scales; small gold crown topped with a crescent moon; pearl necklace with a gold crescent pendant. Baby Lady: small fluffy cocker-spaniel puppy, golden-cream curly ears, dark round eyes; iridescent pearl-white fairy wings, smaller and softer than Moon's; pale silver-pink mermaid tail in place of back legs — she has no back legs and no back paws, her puppy body ends in the mermaid tail, front paws only; tiny gold crescent charm on a pearl collar.
