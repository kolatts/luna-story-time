Daytime on a grassy hillside. High on the hilltop above stands a charcoal-and-violet stone palace with one dark tower on a grassy hilltop, cracked and crumbling. Low in the hillside below it, a big dark arched cave mouth with tree roots dangling over its edge. Exactly Ruby standing at the cave mouth looking in, brave but a little nervous, her silver forehead mark glowing with tiny silver sparkles, with five baby dragons: Flame, Pearl, Sparks, the YELLOW baby dragon with silver eyes, and Crackle, the SILVER baby dragon with yellow eyes, each appearing exactly once, and Luna, the puppy-sized baby BLACK dragon speckled with silver stars huddled around her legs. High up on top of a cracked palace wall, War yells down with her hands cupped round her mouth, and Noir points down crossly from a broken window. Ruby normal plum dress, tiny crown, silver forehead mark visible. Exactly these characters, each appearing once, no other people, no Black Hole. Ruby: a small human girl with the proportions and appearance of a four-year-old child, warm brown skin, shoulder-length wavy rainbow hair with distinct red orange yellow green blue violet strands, shimmering golden eyes, a small flat silver birthmark shaped like an upright side-view dragon silhouette (head at top, curled tail at bottom) centered just above her eyebrows and about as tall as one of her eyes, never a jewel or tiara; a tiny simple gold crown with exactly three ball-tipped points and one small round ruby-red gem on the middle point, only about one third the width of her head, plum-purple tunic dress with gold trim, soft golden boots. She is magically newborn but walks and talks. Always fully human, two human arms and two human legs, no horns, no wings, no tail, no scales on skin. Her magical transformations change her dress and hand magic only; her rainbow hair, golden eyes and silver forehead mark remain. Flame: female baby fire dragon the size of a small puppy, rounded red body, orange cheek frills and tiny orange wings, buttery yellow belly and short yellow horns, large amber eyes, four little legs and one curled red tail, playful snuggly expression. Dragon, never a human. Tiny friendly flame puffs only when specified. Pearl: female baby water dragon the size of a small puppy, pearl-white rounded body, sky-blue ears and wing membranes, blue back ridge and curled blue-tipped tail, large blue eyes, four tiny legs, clear transparent soap-like bubbles floating around her. Dragon, never a human. No fire, no colored opaque bubbles. Sparks: female baby lightning dragon the size of a small puppy, rounded body of bright lemon-YELLOW scales, shining SILVER eyes, tiny silver-edged wings, two short silver horns, a row of small silver zigzag spikes down her back, four little legs and one curled yellow tail ending in a silver zigzag tip, bouncy fizzy expression. She is the yellow twin with silver eyes. Dragon, never a human, never a bird. Tiny gold electric sparkles only when specified. Crackle: female baby lightning dragon the size of a small puppy, rounded body of shining SILVER scales, bright YELLOW eyes, tiny yellow-edged wings, two short yellow horns, a row of small yellow zigzag spikes down her back, four little legs and one curled silver tail ending in a yellow zigzag tip, cheeky crackly grin. She is the silver twin with yellow eyes: same shape as Sparks with the colors swapped. Dragon, never a human, never a bird. Tiny gold electric sparkles only when specified. Luna: female baby moon dragon the size of a small puppy, rounded body of deep glossy BLACK scales speckled all over with tiny twinkling silver-white stars like a night sky, a soft pale-silver belly, large glowing moon-silver eyes, tiny black wings with star-speckled silver edges, two short pearly-silver horns, four little legs and one curled black tail ending in a small round glowing silver tip, sweet sleepy expression. A faint silver moonlight glow rims her outline so she is always clearly visible against the night. Much smaller and rounder than Black Hole, no lavender highlights, no violet wings. Dragon, never a human, never a cat. Silver moonbeam magic only when specified. Noir: adult human stepmother with cool pale skin, straight chin-length black hair, tall narrow silver crown, elegant charcoal gown with violet lining and long sleeves, stern calculating expression. Rounded storybook features, never monstrous. No dragon forehead mark. War: human stepsister appearing eight years old, cool pale skin, black hair in a single high braid, dark burgundy tunic with silver belt, black boots, small angular silver tiara, stubborn scowling expression. No weapons, no dragon forehead mark.
