🏰 Princess Moon's Castle Life

Wander the moonlit grounds with Princess Moon, gather sparkly treasures, and craft cozy things for your castle.

  • 🕹️ Drag the floating circle (or the arrow keys) to walk
  • ✨ Tap the sparkle button (or Space/Enter) to gather, talk, or open something
  • 🎒 Satchel holds what you've gathered
  • 🧵 Crafting turns what's in your satchel into cozy things
  • 🪑 Furniture — tap a piece, then tap a floor tile to place it 💫
