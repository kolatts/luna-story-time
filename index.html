The Bookshelf

How Story Time Works

🔊

Read to me

Press play and the story reads itself out loud, lighting up each word as it goes — like following along with a finger.

👆

Tap any word

Stuck on a word? Tap it and hear it spoken. Every single word in every story is tappable.

✨

Sparkle words

Special words glow pink. Tap one to learn what it means, with a friendly definition made for small ears.

🌙

Wonder together

Every book ends with gentle questions to talk about, and a look-and-find game hidden in the paintings.

Dream Up a Story

What should happen in the next adventure? A brave snail? A cloud that lost its rain? Send your idea to the castle — it might become a real storybook on this very shelf.
